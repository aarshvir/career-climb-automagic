# Jobvance public-page design research

Checked 11 October 2026. Official product homepages were opened through the web tool. The main task also visually inspected all eight rendered public homepages and their public product previews. No competitor account signup was performed; full authenticated products were not evaluated. These observations concern public presentation, examples and information structure, not usability-study results or measured conversion findings. No competitor score, copied creative, copied asset, customer logo or outcome claim is used in Jobvance.

## Rendered public-preview observations

The main task's browser review corroborated the following visual directions on the same official pages: Linear's calm dark window-framed app panels; Notion's large sans headings and light workspace illustration; Teal's tabbed resume/matching preview and single yellow primary action; Huntr's asymmetric type, lavender surfaces and board lifecycle preview; Raycast's dark red product identity and one dominant action; Stripe's ruled grid, accent palette and category depth; Framer's editorial uppercase type and wide product stage; and Jobscan's simple task entry and tabbed match-report preview. These are observations of public marketing demonstrations, not assertions about authenticated usability or product outcomes.

Jobvance applies the underlying clarity and hierarchy through its own cobalt identity and fictional evidence illustration. Teal's interview multiplier, competitor trust logos, reliability metrics and Jobscan's ATS positioning are not adopted as Jobvance claims.

## Design decisions implemented

The palette keeps Jobvance's cobalt `#2850df` with deep ink `#172338`, warm off-white `#f7f8fa`, subtle lavender and restrained lime. Lime carries decorative emphasis or ink text; it is not used as low-contrast body text. System sans typography provides large editorial headings; occasional Georgia italic text supplies a distinct rhythm without adding a font request.

The homepage progresses through an asymmetric introduction and fictional evidence screen, a source-order illustration, a six-step ruled layout, a private-workflow section, a focused pilot invitation and a final free-check action. Every example is marked fictional. There are no invented testimonials, employer logos, usage figures or match percentages. Product previews use real comparison labels and distinguish assistance from leadership.

Secondary pages share the same typography and hierarchy. Pricing retains the free beta and planned $29 pass with a disabled purchase action. All existing legal, refund, help, FAQ and service-availability content is retained, with additional section headings for scanning. Account routing remains conditional on existing authentication; the support mail link remains conditional on its existing valid configuration.

CSS is scoped to `.product-page`. Layouts collapse deliberately at tablet and mobile widths, with a 320px single-column evidence preview. Actions have at least 44px touch targets and visible focus states. Native disclosure controls handle FAQs. Reduced-motion preferences disable transitions. No external fonts, images, new runtime dependencies or decorative animations are introduced.

## Verification and remaining checks

The app TypeScript check and focused ProductPage lint passed. The stylesheet parsed successfully and its rules are explicitly namespaced beneath `.product-page`. Calculated foreground/background ratios for selected meaningful text are: body 5.87:1, primary button 6.36:1, direct-evidence label 7.05:1, related-evidence label 6.67:1, private-panel text 8.54:1 and disabled-pass text 5.32:1. Decorative arrows and circles are hidden from assistive technology.

The main task owns rendered desktop/mobile review, route smoke checks and publication. These checks are not a claim of formal WCAG certification or demonstrated conversion improvement. Public legal wording remains the existing product description; this redesign does not create legal approval or change service availability.
