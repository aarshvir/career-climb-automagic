# Free pilot and fictional demo

The public route `/pilot` introduces a free, guided research pilot for UAE professionals considering strategy/transformation roles and career coaches. It is linked from the header, footer and home page, and included in the sitemap.

The interactive demo uses only Alex Morgan, Example Advisory/Example Group and explicitly fictional source passages. It contains prewritten material, not live AI evaluation or customer data. Visitors can switch role direction, inspect source passages, review a rejected ownership/result overstatement, approve public facts and download a fictional text packet. The packet includes a fictional role brief, supported/not-established requirements, a prewritten recruiter draft and proposed next step. Changing direction or withdrawing a review revokes the previous approval. The fictional restricted internal estimate is never included in an export.

The pilot-request form holds entered details only in page memory. It creates a reviewable draft with copy/download options and an explicitly user-triggered mailto link to the existing Jobvance business contact. It performs no server submission, creates no account or booking, and sends no email. The page states that a prepared draft is not a received request. Initial contact does not request private CVs, employer secrets or client documents.

No billing provider, backend data, account access, outreach automation or merchant settings are changed. Payments remain disabled. Coach prospect research and personalized invitations are maintained separately outside the application repository, as local unsent review artifacts.

`public/pilot-demo-guide.txt` provides a short fictional walkthrough. New focused checks cover approval/reset behavior, safe export, request invalidation, encoded email-body content and no network/storage submission. Browser verification should include desktop/mobile layout, source review, role-change invalidation and draft preparation; do not click email-send or booking actions during QA.
