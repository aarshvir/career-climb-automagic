# Jobvance: ten builds, ten customer archetypes and a VC lens

11 October 2026 · Asia/Dubai · Suggested strategy and acceptance criteria. **These are proposed builds, not a release receipt.**

Build one application-preparation product with ten connected modules. The candidate confirms a reusable career record, chooses a suitable sourced vacancy, reviews an accurate pack and sees a dated next action in one private workspace. Coaches can help with a specific authorized version. A programme can later support successive cohorts if its buyer workflow is validated.

**Recommended first learning wedge:** coach-assisted MBA/experienced-pivot first and second applications, using a candidate-supplied vacancy, source-linked facts, exact review and dated tracking. This is a hypothesis, not proven demand. Begin with B2+B4+B5+B7 and relevant B1 onboarding; fix B10 support/trust first. Students remain an important initial task test, especially for sparse project evidence. Broader discovery and recurring automation then serve employed switchers. Senior, returner and institution workflows require separate validation before expansion.

## The score contract

The owner requests every customer archetype at **95+**, customer consolidation at **98+**, and VC at **95+**. Use these as future acceptance gates, never as grades imposed on unbuilt features:

- Every one of the ten independently reassessed customer readiness scores must be at least 95.
- Their equal-weight mean must be at least 98.
- The separate simulated VC evidence-readiness score must be at least 95.
- If VC is included in the consolidated figure, the eleven-lens mean must also be at least 98: **(sum of ten customer scores + VC score) / 11**.
- At VC = 95, the customer mean needs to be **98.3**, rather than 98, to make that eleven-lens mean 98. At customer mean = 98 and VC = 95, the combined result is only **97.73**.

**No future target above has been achieved or forecast.** Current prior six-lens product readiness is approximately **61/100**, with the original three core audiences at approximately **57/100**. The newly defined ten archetypes do not have ten new live readiness scores. Prior student/MBA scores cannot be copied into their subtypes as newly measured results. [Original audit and evidence boundaries](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md).

Below, feature scores rate **conceptual usefulness assuming correct delivery**. They do not rate current platform quality, future customer satisfaction, willingness to pay or probability of employment. An optional coach module can have low usefulness for a self-directed applicant while the overall journey is excellent. Do not average feature scores into platform readiness or force all cells above 95.

### B1. Personalized entry and target-role brief

**Customer experience:** A landing page, example and starting task suited to their audience, with editable target roles and constraints carried into onboarding.

**Implementation:** Shared audience configuration powers the three initial landing routes and task defaults; add additional paths only after validating distinct needs. Preserve audience through guest navigation and sign-in without putting personal data into URLs.

**Automate:** Carry the selected context forward and suggest relevant starting fields/examples.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Choose or change audience, target direction and constraints.

**Accept when:** Each initial audience enters on phone/desktop, changes choice and signs in without input loss or silent consent; can explain current capabilities.

**Current evidence:** Homepage audience choices exist; distinct planned routes and full carry-through need implementation/verification.

**Proposed owner:** Product designer + frontend engineer. **Dependencies:** B7 draft/state continuity. **Original backlog:** F04, F06, F15 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B2. Reusable verified career record

**Customer experience:** One readable record of employment, projects, skills, dates, contribution, outcome and sharing choices, with every fact linked to its source.

**Implementation:** Parse existing inputs into editable suggestions, start with three guided facts, retain source/version context, track correction/withdrawal dependencies and reuse only confirmed shareable facts.

**Automate:** Parse, suggest, categorize, reuse and identify missing/conflicting information.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Confirm factual accuracy and allowed disclosure; keep confidential or sensitive facts private.

**Accept when:** First and second vacancies use the same record without retyping; team ownership, unfinished work, chronology and projected results retain meaning; account boundaries hold.

**Current evidence:** Cloud record/claim controls exist; guided setup and connected guest reuse remain gaps.

**Proposed owner:** Evidence engineer + full-stack engineer. **Dependencies:** B4 semantics and B10 isolation. **Original backlog:** F01, F06, F07, F20 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B3. Permitted source-backed vacancy discovery

**Customer experience:** A short useful list showing why each role fits, the original posting, checked time, conditions, closing uncertainty and next action.

**Implementation:** Start with permitted employer feeds or maintained source-backed lists. Canonicalize/deduplicate postings; keep full text and source provenance. Separate posting evidence from preference matching and do not treat snippets as verified vacancies.

**Automate:** Fetch permitted sources, deduplicate, extract and rank; label unavailable or incomplete data.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Choose directions, confirm ambiguous conditions and decide which roles to pursue.

**Accept when:** Each target cohort sees genuinely useful sourced roles; closed/incomplete sources are clear; budget-exhausted discovery is distinguishable from zero suitable results.

**Current evidence:** Manual vacancy intake and source adapter code exist; enabled useful external discovery was not demonstrated in the audit.

**Proposed owner:** Integration engineer + source/operator owner. **Dependencies:** Permitted source access, B4 conditions and B10 cost controls. **Original backlog:** F05, F15, F22 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B4. CV-to-vacancy evidence and conditions review

**Customer experience:** Supported evidence, unresolved requirements, qualifications, dates and employer conditions, each with the relevant quote and a useful next question.

**Implementation:** Scope uncertainty to its actual fact/skill; classify conditions separately from achievements; remove metadata noise and keep original sources inspectable. Correct the reproduced Python, sponsorship and date-guidance defects before expanding the funnel.

**Automate:** Extract and organize requirements, find relevant evidence and flag conflicts/unknowns.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Resolve missing facts and employer questions; confirm suitability without invented qualifications.

**Accept when:** Counterfactual and attribution/negation/date fixtures pass; no unrelated quotes support conditions; academic evidence never silently establishes required professional experience.

**Current evidence:** The source-conscious check exists with reproduced precision/guidance defects.

**Proposed owner:** Evidence engineer + careers adviser. **Dependencies:** Source record and complete vacancy text. **Original backlog:** F01, F02, F15, F22 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B5. Exact-preview application-pack studio

**Customer experience:** A real page preview, source-linked change list, safe length/section choices, editable CV, evidence report, letter and reviewed preparation material.

**Implementation:** Bind approval to immutable rendered versions; provide explicit safe shortening/undo; handle output format readiness independently where appropriate; verify mixed-script glyphs/shaping and chosen name forms.

**Automate:** Select confirmed facts, render, validate layout/length and prepare supported formats.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Review exact wording/layout and approve facts and disclosure; select material changes.

**Accept when:** Approved preview matches download; editing revokes affected approval; no silent fact/name loss; a format failure has clear recovery and preserves independently valid outputs.

**Current evidence:** Prior hosted evidence supports generation and hashed downloads; actual layout preview, length recovery and Arabic-name support need improvement.

**Proposed owner:** Document engineer + frontend designer. **Dependencies:** B2/B4 and immutable approval/export controls. **Original backlog:** F09, F10, F17 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B6. Durable opt-in recurring Job Loop

**Customer experience:** A controllable cadence, next run, useful action queue and honest progress, including paused, blocked, failed and recovered work.

**Implementation:** Extend checkpointed cloud runs with explicit schedule/timezone controls and bounded source/model work. Preserve priced Claude/GPT/Kimi adapters, consented recipients, retry reservations, cancellation and rules fallback. Optional inbox needs its own verified permission/setup.

**Automate:** Repeat discovery/preparation, deduplicate work, checkpoint, recover and compile in-app actions.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Opt in, set preferences, pause/cancel, answer essential questions and approve outputs; sending/submission stay user controlled.

**Accept when:** A consented run survives browser closure and interruption without duplicate artifacts/charges; source unavailability and budget holds remain visible; model changes do not bypass consent or the shared cap.

**Current evidence:** User-triggered cloud execution exists in prior release evidence; paid providers/mail are disabled and daily scheduling is absent.

**Proposed owner:** Backend/automation engineer + operator. **Dependencies:** B3 supply, B10 budget/operations; optional external approvals. **Original backlog:** F05, F11, F13, F14, F18, F20 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B7. Private progress workspace and next actions

**Customer experience:** One page with Today, applications, deadlines, exact document versions and a timeline of their actions and automated work.

**Implementation:** Connect check/record/packet/tracking through one application identity. Record substantive actor/time/status/source/version events. Protect drafts, provide clear field recovery and compact mobile review. Keep external application statuses explicitly reported or source-confirmed.

**Automate:** Update real internal progress, dated actions, prepared versions and recovery states.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Review next actions; confirm external submission/interview changes unless a verified integration observed the event.

**Accept when:** First/second applications stay linked without duplicate entry; Privacy/Back, refresh, sign-in and interrupted runs recover; dates respect timezone; no other account can read the workspace.

**Current evidence:** Tracking and cloud opportunity views exist, but continuity is fragmented and guest navigation loss was observed.

**Proposed owner:** Full-stack engineer + product designer. **Dependencies:** B2/B5 version links and B10 isolation. **Original backlog:** F07, F08, F12, F14, F21, F22 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B8. Recruiter and interview preparation

**Customer experience:** A role-specific recruiter brief, draft messages, contribution stories, likely evidence questions and unresolved gaps.

**Implementation:** Reuse approved source facts and posting context. Distinguish aspiration from experience, projected from measured results and individual from team scope. User-supplied/verified contact and thread context remain explicit; no guessed recipients or automatic sending.

**Automate:** Prepare factual drafts, organize stories and suggest relevant review questions.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Choose framing, verify recipients/context and handle conversations/interviews/submission.

**Accept when:** Material claims trace to confirmed facts; withdrawal updates dependent drafts; consulting packet quality never implies case-interview readiness; no unsupported achievements appear.

**Current evidence:** Some letter/draft preparation is present; a coherent recruiter/story preparation journey is proposed.

**Proposed owner:** Evidence/prompt engineer + careers adviser. **Dependencies:** B2/B4/B5 and separate permission for any future integration. **Original backlog:** F17, F15 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B9. Permissioned coach and cohort review

**Customer experience:** Optional scoped review: candidate shares a specific current version, coach comments, candidate corrects/reapproves and can revoke access.

**Implementation:** Begin with a versioned candidate-controlled handoff before a large portal. Add staff/candidate permissions, scoped comments and aggregate programme reporting only after buyer-workflow validation; preserve candidate factual/disclosure authority.

**Automate:** Assemble review bundles and update authorized progress/current versions.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Grant/revoke access, provide adviser judgment, confirm corrections; programme procurement remains a real buyer process.

**Accept when:** Grant/comment/correct/reapprove/revoke works without wrong-version confusion or unauthorized visibility; cohort reports state sources and denominators; adviser comments never silently become confirmed facts.

**Current evidence:** A fictional coach demo/free pilot exists; a shared review/cohort product has not been verified.

**Proposed owner:** Full-stack engineer + coach/programme design partner. **Dependencies:** B2/B5/B7/B10 and evidence of an unmet buyer workflow. **Original backlog:** F16, F19 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

### B10. Reliable low-cost operations and commercial foundation

**Customer experience:** Working support, understandable privacy/control, recoverable failures, accurate availability/limits and a product an operator can maintain.

**Implementation:** Verify support/erasure handling, account isolation, dependency/licence/source inventory, backup/restore and operator handover. Measure useful-pack cost and minimal consent-appropriate funnel/quality events. Keep billing disabled; prepare verified checkout/entitlement/refund/reconciliation only for later eligible launch.

**Automate:** Budget reservations/reconciliation, safe validation, operational monitoring and minimal progress metrics.

**Candidate/operator decisions:** Operate support, resolve incidents/rights/account eligibility and make informed commercial decisions.

**Accept when:** Known trust/recovery defects close; isolation/withdrawal/recovery and cap boundaries are freshly tested; support and restore fulfill actual rehearsals; ownership/transfer paths and costs are evidenced.

**Current evidence:** Significant technical controls and tests exist; support, production recovery, channel economics, ownership/transfer diligence and commercial proof remain incomplete/unverified.

**Proposed owner:** Operator/founder + full-stack/security engineer. **Dependencies:** Verified operational ownership, source rights and later merchant eligibility. **Original backlog:** F03, F04, F14, F18, F19, F20 in the [audit](audits/2026-10-11/Jobvance-audit-report.md#improvement-backlog--owner-evidence-and-acceptance).

## What each archetype must demonstrate before passing

### A. Final-year graduate applicant

**Essential task:** Find a plausible graduate role and turn completed academic/internship evidence into a truthful first application.

**Highest-priority builds:** B4, B3, B5. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Graduation, start-date, professional-experience and sponsorship conditions are separate from CV wording; unknowns remain visible.
- Coursework and projects never silently satisfy required professional experience; the Python counterfactual is stable.
- Original vacancy source and checked time remain inspectable; exact one-page preview matches export and source deadline appears in tracking.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** Permitted sources contain enough suitable graduate roles, and conditions review helps selection rather than merely adding warnings.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Observe first and second applications using fictional facts and permitted postings; compare normal preparation with the prototype, counterbalance order, record assistance/corrections and all abandoned tasks.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Offer optional reuse during a fourteen-day study with genuinely relevant manually checked roles; distinguish study-prompted visits from voluntary return.

### B. Internship seeker with little experience

**Essential task:** Identify defensible contributions from projects, coursework and volunteering without inventing employment or impact.

**Highest-priority builds:** B4, B2, B3. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- A person with no employment history can complete the record and export without manufactured leadership, employment or numbers.
- Individual contribution, team work, completion and unfinished study retain their original meaning.
- Audience/sign-in changes preserve inputs without granting consent; phone correction and draft recovery work.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** A short guided interview exposes evidence users understand and can defend without making onboarding burdensome.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Test sparse fictional records with consenting internship seekers; inspect comprehension and approved facts for invented experience or ownership.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Test enrollment, graduation-window and start-date conditions against source snapshots and ask users to explain known, unknown and next action.

### C. MBA consulting applicant

**Essential task:** Produce a tightly edited, defensible packet and stay ahead of a concentrated recruiting timetable.

**Highest-priority builds:** B5, B7, B2. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Chosen page length follows explicit bullet/section selection, without silently dropping facts or context.
- Work, internships and classroom projects remain distinct; projected savings do not become measured results.
- Dated application/interview milestones and a second employer packet reuse confirmed facts; stories do not imply case-interview readiness.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** Packet editing and the timetable materially improve the existing document/calendar routine.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Compare two fictional employer packets with the existing method; measure relevance, attribution, editing effort and preview/export equivalence.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Run a recruiting-calendar comprehension task with timezone/unknown deadlines and inspect whether users can recover after interruption.

### D. MBA industry/finance/product pivot applicant

**Essential task:** Choose a target direction and translate existing experience into role-specific evidence without inventing a bridge.

**Highest-priority builds:** B4, B1, B2. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Separate target directions have explicit priorities, rather than one blended keyword score.
- MBA coursework remains distinguishable from professional/technical experience requirements.
- Two directions yield meaningfully different evidence selections from one record; confidential or withdrawn facts stay excluded.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** Direction guidance identifies an actionable bridge, rather than producing polished packets for materially unsuitable roles.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Use one fictional career record for two different directions and test evidence-selection explanations with consenting pivot candidates.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Compare recruiter briefs built from the same facts; inspect whether candidates understand aspiration versus demonstrated qualification.

### E. Busy employed midcareer switcher

**Essential task:** Prepare the next relevant application with little repeated effort and clear discretion controls.

**Highest-priority builds:** B2, B6, B7. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- A second vacancy reuses confirmed facts, with new/changed material surfaced for review.
- Employer exclusions have an enforced documented scope; confidential claims are inspectable before sharing.
- Opt-in runs survive browser closure, recover without duplicate packs/charges, expose paused/failed/unavailable states and preserve phone/sign-in progress.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** A short action queue replaces current work instead of becoming another dashboard to maintain.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Five consenting switchers compare normal versus reused first/second-packet tasks with fictional facts; measure active minutes, repeated fields and corrections.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Simulate a week including closed roles, budget/source holds and changed facts; test recovery, state comprehension and pause controls without sends or live integrations.

### F. Senior manager/director/executive

**Essential task:** Express leadership scope accurately across a selected CV, leadership brief and interview evidence.

**Highest-priority builds:** B2, B5, B8. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Personal ownership, team contribution, organisational outcomes, scope and dates remain distinct; supported never becomes led.
- Output type/length is explicitly selected; seniority and confidential commercial metrics are not invented.
- Manager, director and executive tasks are validated separately; confirmed facts alone do not prove a persuasive narrative.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** Factual selection can support leadership framing without extensive additional candidate judgment.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Test synthetic records containing ambiguous scope, shared results and confidential metrics with qualified reviewers across seniority levels.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Blindly compare generic and senior-focused fictional packs for fidelity, relevance, seniority signalling and correction effort.

### G. Career returner after an employment gap

**Essential task:** Build an honest target-role narrative with accurate chronology and optional gap disclosure.

**Highest-priority builds:** B1, B2, B8. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Historical results never become recent achievements, and employment continuity is not manufactured.
- Gap explanation is optional; sensitive personal reasons are not required to complete the workflow.
- Current skill evidence, recent study and employer conditions are distinct; exact dates and disclosure choices survive exports.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** Preparation is a major pain alongside role direction, confidence and recent evidence, rather than a faster generic CV missing the need.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Five consenting returners use fictional dated records and optional gap explanations; test completion without forced disclosure or invented continuity.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Test known/unknown eligibility and next-action comprehension using permitted posting snapshots and fictional records.

### H. UAE international/relocation applicant

**Essential task:** Create usable mixed-script documents and dated actions while distinguishing employer conditions from assumptions.

**Highest-priority builds:** B4, B5, B7. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Chosen Arabic/Latin name forms survive glyphs, shaping, direction, rendering and copy/paste in each supported format.
- Analysis language is stated; mixed-script names do not imply fully bilingual CV analysis.
- Employer-stated location, sponsorship, pay currency/period and dates stay quoted; unknowns remain unknown, and Dubai date-only actions behave correctly.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** Permitted sources contain enough relevant roles with useful conditions detail; broad UAE labels and substrings are insufficient.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Test synthetic mixed-script names, long lines and punctuation across preview, PDF, DOCX, text extraction and copy/paste.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Five consenting participants interpret unknown sponsorship, remote locations, pay periods and Dubai dates from permitted source snapshots without inferred eligibility.

### I. Independent career coach/adviser

**Essential task:** Review accurate current packets with less reconstruction and version confusion.

**Highest-priority builds:** B4, B2, B5. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Source quotations, exact document version and factual/disclosure approvals remain linked in a review bundle.
- Candidate grants explicit scoped access, corrects facts, renews approval and can revoke the coach's access.
- Coach comments never become candidate-attested facts automatically; source/approval history is clear.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** A source-linked review handoff saves enough adviser time to replace email/document reconciliation.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Five consenting coaches review matched fictional packets with and without evidence bundles; record review time, version confusion and seeded material errors.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Test grant, comment, correct, reapprove and revoke in a prototype; all participants must understand what is visible and locate the current version.

### J. University/MBA careers programme buyer

**Essential task:** Support successive cohorts with reliable review and useful reporting without heavy administration.

**Highest-priority builds:** B10, B4, B9. **Desired readiness floor:** 95+, pending fresh evaluation.

- Role-scoped staff/candidate permissions, accessibility, support and reporting scope are tested.
- Cohort data identifies actual activity and candidate-reported outcomes, with consent and accurate denominators.
- A real workflow owner, budget/procurement route and continuation decision are verified; consumer usefulness alone is insufficient.

**Biggest unverified assumption:** An institution has a costly unmet task and realistic adoption path despite existing careers software.

**Low-cost experiment 1:** Interview five consenting actual buyers about their existing tools and one costly workflow; require independent evidence of a common task, owner and evaluation path.

**Low-cost experiment 2:** Test minimal cohort administration with fictional participants; a funded small pilot and renewal test can occur later only after eligibility and buyer requirements are resolved.

Experiments are proposals. No participants have been contacted, interviews run, payments collected or external integrations enabled by this review. Use consenting/inbound participants or separately authorized recruiting; maintain the existing no-outreach instruction. Synthetic records and permitted posting snapshots can test mechanics without private CVs or paid calls. They do not validate real willingness to pay or natural hiring outcomes.

## A VC's end-to-end viewing script

This is a future review script, not a claim that a real investor or this reviewer executed these private actions:

1. Enter a relevant landing page; identify exactly what works now, the audience, the operator and the free offer.
2. Complete a clearly fictional first application: inspect input/source conditions, confirm actual facts and privacy choices.
3. Open the exact prepared document, follow a claim back to its source, make a correction and observe renewed approval.
4. Confirm the correct version is downloaded and linked to a truthful application timeline; employer-link opening does not imply submission.
5. Prepare a second role with the same record; observe candidate minutes and review changes rather than repeat setup.
6. Inspect a configured permitted source and an explicitly unavailable source; assess useful supply, freshness and honest uncertainty.
7. Inspect a consented recurring run, cancellation, failure/recovery and budget hold; verify supported work persists after browser closure.
8. On a phone, navigate to privacy/help and back, correct a field and recover the saved task.
9. With an explicitly authorized coach, inspect scope, comments, exact version, renewed approval and revocation.
10. Inspect real evidence for support/erasure/restore, unit costs, target-user repeat value and acquisition. Then ask a second operator to reproduce the handover with fictional data.

### What would make the business more convincing

- Observed first/second task usefulness and voluntary repeat use at equal source/output quality.
- Comparative adviser/candidate correction and time evidence, with failed and assisted tasks included.
- Permitted vacancy supply, actual channel economics, support load and fully costed useful-pack economics.
- Validated coach/programme workflow owner and procurement/continuation evidence where that channel is targeted.
- Fresh task/isolation/withdrawal/recovery tests plus source/licence/IP/account ownership and transfer/migration verification.
- Eligible arrangements and verified billing/refund/reconciliation when commercial launch is later authorized.

An investor assesses opportunity size, durable differentiation, repeatable distribution, economics and execution. An acquirer additionally needs assets they can lawfully obtain and operate. These are related but separate tests; a transferable small business need not have venture-scale growth. A UI score cannot resolve them. A 95+ score is an aspiration for this declared rubric, not a universal investor threshold.

**Transfer inventory:** source/Git history; domain/DNS; hosting; Supabase database/storage; support mailbox; provider projects; OAuth configuration; source/feed permissions; code/font/template licences; contribution/IP assignments; candidate-data purpose/access/retention; backup/restore and account migration/transfer procedures. Verify actual owner authority and provider paths rather than assuming credentials can simply be sold. Private candidate histories are not automatically a transferable training dataset or a defensible moat. Entity/merchant/contract readiness remains unresolved under the owner's current preferences. No legal acquisition-readiness certification is made.

### Distribution and monetization hypotheses

Start with a free, narrow preparation pilot and optional adviser review. Test whether coaches save correction/reconciliation time and whether candidates reuse facts for a second suitable vacancy. Existing inbound or separately consented participants can support observation without sending outreach. Do not build a broad institution portal before identifying an actual costly unmet programme workflow and its owner.

A job search is episodic: finding employment can correctly end use. Measure useful repeated applications while a person is searching; distinguish successful exit from abandonment. Coach/programme customers may serve successive cohorts, but their procurement, support and permission costs are unproven. Later, when commercial eligibility is resolved, test a search-period pass or cohort/coach offer against actual continuation behaviour. Planned prices and expressions of interest do not establish revenue or willingness to pay.

Feature copying is easy. Potential differentiation is less reconstruction across source-linked facts, exact versions, corrections and candidate/adviser handoffs, with demonstrably lower effort and error at equivalent quality. That advantage must be compared with existing tools. Multiple models and generic prompts do not by themselves establish defensibility.

## The 98+ acceptance process

Use the original ten readiness dimensions: audience/task need, onboarding, evidence/output, interface, complete workflow, useful vacancy supply, tracking, privacy/control, mobile/accessibility and repeat/commercial readiness. Predeclare the concrete task and scoring interpretation for each context before testing; an institution's review/reporting task differs from a candidate's own application. Keep optional functions optional. Do not change weights afterward to make a target pass.

**Release blockers regardless of average:** invented or misattributed material facts; silent name/content loss; cross-account or unauthorized adviser access; ineffective withdrawal; unrecoverable navigation loss without a deliberate discard choice; false sent/applied/completed states; misleading source/provider availability; unbounded spending; and an unusable privacy/support route. A high average cannot compensate for a failed blocker.

**Initial learning study:** ten consenting people across the current three core audiences, using phone and desktop tasks, first and second application preparation and deliberate interruption. Record source/output quality, active minutes, assistance, corrections, abandonment, false assumptions and useful repeat actions. A small exploratory pilot supports decisions, not exceptional market-wide scores.

**Broader validation:** independently observe and reassess all ten contexts after their relevant scope exists, including actual adviser/programme buyers. Use real authorized tasks after synthetic-fixture tests. Publish sample sizes, denominators, failures, exclusions and evidence bounds. A fresh authenticated fictional-account audit must also verify isolation, changed/withdrawn facts after later application stages, exact downloads, interrupted work, formats, support/erasure and recovery. Test configured external calls with bounded consent before claiming them operational.

**Automation measurement:** first setup and repeated tasks have separate baselines. Count active candidate minutes and manual interventions at equivalent quality; include approval, corrections and exception handling. Background runtime and a count of automated micro-tasks are not minutes saved. Proposed pilot goals remain initial straightforward setup within ten active minutes, subsequent straightforward review within three, and no duplicate fact entry. These are targets, not results. Candidate judgment, truthful attestation, disclosure approval, interviews and submission remain substantive work.

**Economic measurement:** the shared US$50/month ceiling covers model/search usage, not hosting, support, subscriptions or tax. Ten users × one run/day × thirty days = 300 runs: allowance averages $0.1667/run before any reserve. This is arithmetic, not observed cost or a paid-provider price quote. Measure cost per useful approved pack, including failed searches, uncertain calls, retries, abandoned packs and support minutes. Rules/manual-source fallback should remain usable when paid capacity is unavailable. No accounts are funded or enabled by this plan.

**Final decision:** calculate the ten customer readiness scores and VC rubric only from declared evidence. Report actual values even if they fail 95/98. Building features creates the opportunity to pass; it does not guarantee the scores, employment, retention, investor interest or a sale. Full commercial evidence requires later eligible billing/procurement and real operations; a free product can improve task quality while those business gates remain pending.
