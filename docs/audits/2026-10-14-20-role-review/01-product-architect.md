## Findings and concrete changes

**PA-01 — P1: complete the free-check-to-Loop handoff.** Source-confirmed: `/check` saves `cv_checks`, while “Also add vacancy” writes a separate `tracked_applications` entry with no vacancy URL (`CvCheck.tsx:322–337`). `/applications` routes to the existing dashboard (`MvpApp.tsx:138–141`). Loop imports only `cv_documents` (`job-loop-api.ts:136–141`; `cv-documents.ts:85–87`). A candidate can therefore finish the promised first application and still need to re-enter its CV and vacancy to reach reusable facts. Add “Continue this application in Job Loop” with a reviewable, consented import of the chosen CV version, vacancy and source reference. Keep imported claims unconfirmed and not shareable. Acceptance: each persona completes a guest check, signs in, sees the exact selected texts in Loop, confirms facts, and finds one linked application record; retry creates no duplicate. Dependencies: owner-bound import contract and an additive association, not destructive table consolidation. **2–4 engineering days**, no additional model cost.

**PA-02 — P1: separate factual corrections from application-specific composition.** Source-confirmed: Loop’s final CV field is read-only and directs every factual edit through the career record and rebuild (`JobLoop.tsx:404`). `LoopAction` exposes no packet composition draft (`job-loop-types.ts:110–127`). This protects truthfulness but gives a busy candidate no local way to reorder or omit approved material for one role. Add a small per-application selection/order draft using complete, confirmed claim IDs; preserve required employment/education context and immutable facts. New factual wording should still return to claim confirmation. Acceptance: shorten application B without changing the library or approval of A; changed selection revokes B’s approval; context cannot be silently lost. Dependencies: versioned selection hash, renderer and existing approval gates. **3–5 days**, rules-based by default. This is a usability hypothesis, not an observed abandonment claim.

**PA-03 — P1: let candidates choose the next bounded preparation.** Source-confirmed: `start_run` accepts provider/consent, not selected vacancies (`job-loop-types.ts:118`); selection prioritizes manual records, then saved status and heuristic score, taking two (`job-loop-runtime.ts:16–20`). With three vacancies, the candidate cannot explicitly choose the urgent one. Add a “Prepare these 1–2 vacancies” preview showing the original source, unresolved conditions and expected method before a manual run. Acceptance: selected IDs are owner-validated and hash-bound; a due vacancy can be prepared first; overflow is visible; unchanged packets reuse safely. Scheduling may retain automatic ranking as an explicit opt-in. Dependencies: run snapshot/idempotency and capacity validation. **1.5–3 days**, avoids spending scarce calls on unwanted work.

**PA-04 — P1: define the pilot’s learning decision.** Existing metrics already distinguish requested downloads from completed downloads and scheduled runs from candidate returns (`20261012181402_jobvance_admin_snapshot.sql:59–86`). They cannot answer whether reuse makes a second useful application easier. Specify a ten-user research protocol: initial real vacancy, a second distinct vacancy within seven days, active candidate minutes, assistance needed, candidate-reported usable artifact and dated next action. Record results manually with consent; keep sample size and persona visible. Acceptance: every participant has a baseline and repeat-task record or an explicit non-completion reason; machine activity never counts as user return; no inferred hiring claim. Dependencies: researcher ownership and agreed observation definitions. **0.5–1.5 days** for minimal recording, plus research time; no paid telemetry service required. Proposed targets are hypotheses, not launch guarantees.

**PA-05 — P2: consolidate the product authority.** `CLOUD-JOB-LOOP-CONTRACT.md:9` describes five opportunities and three search queries, while `job-loop-providers.ts:9` implements two packets and two queries. `FREE-PILOT.md:3` targets UAE professionals/coaches while current `/pilot` includes students and MBA job seekers. Create one dated capability/requirements matrix linking routes, source operations, configuration dependencies and acceptance checks; mark superseded contracts. Clarify that “MBA” currently means job recruiting during/after an MBA (`job-loop-audiences.ts:15–24`); admissions applications are outside the vacancy-based journey unless explicitly respecified. Acceptance: public/help/spec examples agree on pilot scope and configured availability. **0.5–1 day**; dependency: product-owner decision.

## Shared stories and first week

The shared stories are: **bring one CV and vacancy without signup; transfer reviewed work with consent; reuse confirmed facts for a second chosen vacancy; approve a specific document and keep a dated action.** Student project context, MBA academic/work distinctions and professional confidentiality are acceptance variants of these stories.

Order week one as: agree the pilot outcome and canonical boundary; implement the consented handoff and vacancy choice; prototype document-specific selection; run the same first/second-application task with participants from all three groups. Keep paid calls optional under the existing shared ceiling.

Do not build now: separate persona products, MBA admissions essays, institution/coach portals, full interview coaching, automatic employer submissions, recruiter sending, broad scraper coverage, paid checkout, or predictive ATS/hiring scores. The next decision is whether the shared workflow delivers repeat value with tolerable human review, not whether the feature catalogue is larger.
