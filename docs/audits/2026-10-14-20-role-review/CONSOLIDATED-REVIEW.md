# Jobvance: twenty-role review and product plan

**14 October 2026 · Private owner report · All twenty named reviews complete**

Jobvance has a usable public CV-to-vacancy check and substantial safeguards behind its private workflow. The next investment should protect completed work and make the first application carry naturally into the next one. Wider promotion should follow verified private onboarding, understandable background-run results and observed repeat usefulness.

The reviews do **not** establish best-in-class status, a 95/98 product score, profitable demand, improved hiring outcomes or search-ranking leadership. They establish specific strengths, reproducible defects, missing operating evidence and a plan to test the central proposition.

Twenty separate role contexts reviewed one fixed snapshot in waves under the four-concurrent-agent limit. Their 88 original findings map to 19 coordinator work packages. These packages are a sequenced backlog, not 19 simultaneous projects. No application changes, release, ads, outreach, optional emails, new paid model calls or service purchases occurred in this round. The live version remains unchanged.

## What works, and what the evidence actually covers

- **Public first value:** a candidate can try the six-step check without signup or an external model call. Live fictional testing reached evidence, review, a one-page PDF preview and download preparation. Editing the reviewed document revoked approval/export readiness. The sampled 390-pixel mobile journey had no horizontal overflow. Final saved-file bytes and opened Word/PDF layout still need a repeatable hosted release check.
- **Factual controls:** source passages, academic/team context, exact approval, source revisions and private content-addressed artifacts provide a useful foundation. Private code has ownership, freshness, lease, cancellation and idempotency controls. This is source/local-test evidence; it does not certify every production boundary.
- **Reuse already exists in parts:** the public check has a repeat-application path, and unchanged private packets can be reused. The major gap is continuity between completed checks, separate saved CV stores, tracking and the private Loop. It would be inaccurate to say reuse is entirely absent.
- **Spending containment:** candidate and administrator processing share an atomic monthly ledger. Unknown calls retain reservations. Real-money checkout is deliberately disabled and the UI identifies proposed prices as future plans.
- **Help/content foundation:** task guides, five narrated screen-walkthrough asset sets, transcripts/captions and public help/contact exist. Media existence was source-checked; this round did not independently certify every playback/caption timing. Existing fictional/AI/simulation disclosures must survive asset reuse.
- **Search foundations:** sampled public pages had useful HTML, canonicals and structured data; the sitemap lists 38 public URLs. Private `/loop` was noindex and an invented route returned 404. Indexability is distinct from actual indexing, traffic, citations or rank; none of those outcomes was verified.

Prior release evidence reports 899 tests across 66 files. Reviewers also ran bounded role-specific subsets and probes. Those runs overlap; their totals must not be added into a fictitious new unique-test count. The coordinator independently reran two frontend observations, three backend observations and the local SQL erasure reproduction. Observation tests deliberately assert the defective current outcome, so a passing observation is a reproduced bug, not a successful desired behavior.

## Highest-value findings

**1. Protect work already done.** Two synthetic frontend cases reproduce silent loss: a signed-in recovery draft is removed while authentication is loading, and returning through an unchanged vacancy/recheck replaces final CV edits with source wording. Existing tracking edits are also outside recovery protection. An optional save shares global busy state with local exports without an application deadline. Fix ownership-aware hydration, document transition rules and independent save/export progress before adding more steps. Sources: FE-01/02/03, QA-01, UX-04 → **JV-01**.

**2. Complete erasure under concurrency.** A local SQL reproduction starts erasure, reenrolls/starts a run, uploads after the prefix scan, then finishes erasure: one private object remains while record/run rows are gone. This is a retention race, not demonstrated public exposure. Separately, more than 10,000 scanned entries can repeatedly fail before any removal. Use durable erasing state/generation, a mutation/storage fence, resumable bounded deletion, final empty-prefix verification and safe orphan reconciliation. Sources: DB-01, BACKEND-02, CS-02 → **JV-02**.

**3. Make “continue this application” a real handoff.** A completed free check does not import directly into reusable Loop facts/opportunities. Allow a preview of the chosen CV version and vacancy across sign-in, preserve storage consent, and keep imported claims unconfirmed until reviewed. Let the candidate choose which one or two saved vacancies to prepare. Sources: PA-01/03, UX-01 → **JV-03**.

**4. Tailor the application without rewriting the career record.** Private Loop review is read-only; changing emphasis currently points back to shared facts and rebuilding. Add per-opportunity complete-claim selection/order, context-preservation rules and a composition hash. Keep factual correction in the source-confirmation path. Constrain interview prompts too: a local mocked adapter accepted an invented managing-director/global-merger presupposition against coursework evidence, although literal CV facts remain separately guarded. Sources: PA-02, UX-02, BACKEND-03 → **JV-04**.

**5. Prove the seams in the deployed journey.** Component tests do not establish ordinary-address confirmation/reset delivery, real redirect timing, final downloaded bytes, complex document reading order or opened Word/PDF layout. Add a small controlled hosted smoke and fictional document corpus; record unavailable configuration honestly. Source review also found PDF processing needs cumulative limits/deadline/cancellation. Sources: API-02, EMAIL-02, QA-03/04, SEC-02 → **JV-06**.

**6. Show actual background results and causes.** Scheduler readiness currently reflects dispatch attempts/configuration, rather than worker acknowledgement and useful progress. Historical run presentation can derive a label from today's model configuration. “Partial” combines expected unavailable lanes and genuine faults; current funnel stages also require a completed run before counting approval/download, so valid packet value from partial runs can disappear from that funnel. Add immutable receipts, safe correlation references, distinct outcome dimensions and independent task milestones. Sources: API-01, DEVOPS-02, BT-01/02/03, R18-03 → **JV-09/11**.

**7. Finish support closure.** Guest receipts support a reply/readback path; signed-in Loop support currently exposes status-only handling. Add a versioned private reply and next step, atomic idempotent intake and oldest-unresolved visibility for both queues. Help needs ordinary-language aliases for “resume upload,” “lost draft,” password and cost holds. Sources: CS-01/03/04, DB-04, TW-02/03, PL-04 → **JV-10**.

**8. Make release recovery executable.** All 237 entries in the existing backend manifest matched inspected source/artifact bytes, but the manifest omits the guest-support migration. An auxiliary testcodex Pages workflow swallows build failures and can choose `public` after `dist`; its production impact was not established. The generic operations guide contains nonexistent commands and conflicts with current instructions. Frontend rollback exists; private-data/file restoration remains unproven. Sources: DEVOPS-01/03/04/05, QA-02, TW-01, BT-04 → **JV-08**.

Sensitive administrator actions also need enforced recent strong authentication (SEC-01); this finding is a hardening gap, not a demonstrated outsider administrator bypass. Public intake needs a verified trusted-ingress identity contract (SEC-03), not an assumption that a spoofed header currently succeeds. Both map to **JV-07**.

## One product for the three audiences

The common workflow should be:

**CV or project evidence → one actual vacancy → visible source comparison → per-application composition → exact review/approval → usable download → saved next action → second vacancy with prior work retained.**

| Audience | Particular problem | Shared feature and acceptance example |
|---|---|---|
| Student/new graduate | Limited employment history; project evidence needs explanation. | Retain no-CV starter and academic context. A project is not silently rewritten as commercial ownership. |
| MBA student/alumnus seeking a job | Translate prior work/coursework into a pivot while preserving team attribution. | Reuse evidence, then select relevant complete passages for each role. A forecast or team result retains its status and individual contribution. |
| Busy 5–10-year professional | Little preparation time; confidential details and repeated corrections must stay removed. | Persist reviewed facts and disclosure choices, select a priority vacancy, preserve final edits and compare first/second active effort. |

These are product hypotheses and source-derived needs, not findings from three completed live customer studies. Do not build separate applications, onboarding databases or AI agents for each audience. Change entry-page examples/defaults and the relevant evidence explanation. Automate parsing, reuse, selection assistance, rendering, scheduling and diagnostics while retaining meaningful factual/disclosure approval and employer-send control. No evidence currently supports a 99% automation claim.

## Pilot protocol and decision rules

Keep a minimal owner-held research sheet: anonymous participant ID, segment/source, permission, task dates, mode, first/repeat eligible/completed, active minutes, assistance minutes, material factual corrections, usable export confirmation, usefulness and missing/withdrawal reason. Keep CV/employer/contact text out of it. Use the current shared data boundaries and explicit research choice; do not silently identify guests across visits.

For the first task, use the participant's actual CV/project evidence and one real vacancy. For a second distinct vacancy 7–14 days later, observe whether the same reviewed work carries forward. Pause active-time measurement during unrelated interruptions. Ask whether the exported file is usable as is, usable with minor corrections, needs major rework or unusable. Record a deliberate participant action separately from a scheduled system run. An approved/download-link request remains a proxy unless the participant confirms a usable opened file.

Adopt rules before looking at results. The launch/lead reviewers propose **six usable first tasks and four voluntary repeat tasks out of ten** as a provisional reason to continue learning, not a launch benchmark. For broad expansion, use a stricter internal hypothesis of at least **eight usable completions**, no repeated unresolved failure in one audience and cleared work-preservation/erasure/auth/restore gates. Publish denominators and missingness internally. A sample of ten does not establish statistical superiority, persona rankings, hiring effect or validated conversion.

If three participants encounter the same material failure, pause recruitment for that task and fix it. Separate active search, voluntary pause, accepted role, stopped and unknown; getting a job should not automatically look like failed retention. Founder/staff/demo/QA/unknown accounts require explicit server-owned cohort classification; domain patterns alone do not define genuine customers.

For comparative research, start with identical fictional material on two close alternatives and the existing document workflow. Any real candidate content needs separate informed permission for the other service. Compare first task, second vacancy, corrected fact and withdrawn confidential detail. Record effort, corrections, lost edits and usable outputs. Do not infer competitor safeguards are absent because their marketing pages do not mention them.

For an MBA jobseeker, test team/project attribution. For a professional, test retained confidentiality choices and reduced repeat preparation effort. These may become a reason to switch, but there is no demonstrated moat or universal superiority yet.

Maintain one manual monthly comparison row: vendor, task, official URL, date, advertised/tested/unknown, free limit, price/cadence/region, what changed, consequence, owner and next check. Trigger an earlier review when free exports/autofill, source-linked approvals, AI/deletion terms or a pilot participant's named alternative changes. A 30–45 minute monthly review is sufficient as a planning assumption; no competitor-monitoring subscription was activated.

## A concrete prepared growth sequence

1. **Fix the promise and destination.** Demonstrations lead to the no-account`/check` with the appropriate audience; explanatory reuse content leads to`/job-loop`; intentional saved work leads to`/loop`. Keep guided concept research on`/pilot` explicitly identified. Prepare one current pilot brief stating input, deliverable, assistance and limits.
2. **Qualify research participants.** Separate students, MBA recruiting/alumni relationships and existing professional relationships. Coaches remain potential introduction partners. Verify their current public service and ask about recurring relevant work only during later authorized discovery; a 20-business list does not establish willingness or participant availability. No prospect was contacted.
3. **Improve four existing guides.** Focus on CV-vacancy evidence, student projects, MBA pivots and second-application reuse. Add one shared worksheet: requirement → literal source → context/contribution → status/uncertainty → approved wording → selected application → next action. Add two relevant guide links to each audience page and true per-guide dates. Avoid another batch of generic articles before learning from the current library.
4. **Prepare one original 35-second demonstration.** Seconds 0–5: vacancy requirement. 5–12: enlarged fictional source passage. 12–20: show an overstatement and a context-preserving version, explicitly labelled as a prepared comparison. 20–29: actual reviewed preview/approval/opened file after fixes. 29–35: second-vacancy reuse and one`/check`action. Retain captions, readable crops and fictional/AI labels. Adapt the source example for each audience; do not present prewritten footage as a live AI result or copy someone else's reel.
5. **Prepare a small channel test.** Social's first hypothesis is two LinkedIn posts/week for two weeks using the demonstration, evidence image, persona example and reuse explanation. Confirm actual audience response before Instagram expansion. Record source, qualified conversations, task completion, assistance and repeat work. Impressions alone are not useful-product evidence. This plan is unsent/unscheduled.
6. **Prepare a later launch kit.** Include factual description, logo, fictional screenshots, captioned demo/transcript, support route and current limits. Product Hunt or eligible Show HN can be tested sequentially after pilot gates. [BetaList's current FAQ](https://betalist.com/faq) says submissions are paid, so it is excluded under this round's no-paid-launch constraint. No submissions occurred.
7. **Keep optional email as drafts.** The email review includes four task-based draft examples. Activate nothing now. Before later optional sending, build purpose-specific permission, unsubscribe/suppression, task completion/pause stop rules and a discreet local send window. Authentication delivery is a separate operational need. A signup, pilot inquiry or preparation consent is not a marketing subscription.

SEO work should improve useful answers and connect them to a working task. Search Console/Bing observations, consent-appropriate first-party counts and actual citations are needed to assess results. Publishing 20 pages, adding schema or allowing a search crawler does not guarantee a Google position or a citation in Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude. AI-detector percentages and synthetic “human team” claims are not product acceptance criteria.

## What stays deferred

Keep the coach/institution portal B9, separate persona products, auto-applying, employer/recruiter sending, broad unauthorized scraping, full interview/case coaching, paid ads, directory blasts, annual/unlimited billing and new paid analytics/chat/monitoring suites outside the next release. Optional email/Gmail and recurring payment work have explicit prerequisites. A paid avatar overlay is not needed to validate the task; current screen help must first match the real product and remain accessible.

Automated operations should classify/deduplicate issues, show recoverable states and perform explicitly safe idempotent work. Code fixes need a reproducible case, relevant regression checks, a reviewed release artifact and readback/rollback evidence under the owner's existing authorization. They must not raise the cap, invent a successful run, approve a candidate document, erase unrelated data or blindly replay uncertain paid calls.

## How to judge the next round

Re-audit the exact released version after fixes. Require zero observed silent-work-loss cases in the defined auth/navigation corpus, passing erasure concurrency/resumability cases, controlled hosted auth/file/restore evidence, usable support readback and correct cap/uncertainty behavior. Then add actual candidate first/repeat-task observations and explicit non-completion reasons. Re-score each role against its own evidence. Some commercial/growth grades should remain limited until real value and operating data exist.

A higher grade must follow evidence; completing a checklist does not mathematically produce 95+. From an investor's perspective, the remaining sellability evidence is a consistent task advantage, voluntary repeat use, switching reasons, support-adjusted unit economics and a transferable release/operating process. This is a coordinator synthesis of the product, analytics, finance and competitive reviews, not a separately commissioned VC opinion or valuation.
