## Findings and acceptance

**BACKEND-01 — P2: definitely rejected provider requests consume uncertain budget.** Source-confirmed and locally observed. `jobvance-openrouter.ts:62–63` distinguishes a pre-provider rejection with no request ID or usage and throws `ROUTER_REQUEST_REJECTED`. However, `job-loop-runtime.ts:175–179` marks every failure other than disabled search uncertain. The probe fed a mocked HTTP429 with only an error payload; settlement received `(usageId, null, null, true)`. This retains the complete reservation even though the adapter expressly classified no model usage. Repeated authentication, funding or rate-limit failures can strand the shared pilot allowance without equivalent spend. Preserve the conservative treatment of genuinely ambiguous calls, but settle definite pre-provider rejections to zero and define explicit retry eligibility after configuration repair. Acceptance: known400/401/402/403/404/429 rejection fixtures release only their own reservation; timeouts/malformed usage remain reserved; retry creates at most one new authorized provider attempt. Dependencies: provider error taxonomy, reserve/settle replay contract. Estimate: **1–2 engineering days**. Cost effect: avoids artificial budget exhaustion; no extra service required.

**BACKEND-02 — P2: artifact erasure has a permanent no-progress boundary.** Source-confirmed and locally observed. `job-loop-repository.ts:75–88` scans the whole account before removing any objects, throwing after10,000 entries. Two probe retries over10,001 synthetic object rows both threw `LOOP_ERASURE_RETRY`; neither called storage removal or final erasure. The message suggests retry, but an unchanged account cannot progress. Separately, `job-loop-process.test.ts:32–35` explicitly verifies that cancelled rendering leaves uploaded artifacts, and runtime `265–268` uploads before fenced commit. The examined code contains no age-based orphan collection. Implement resumable bounded deletion that records progress, and reconcile unreferenced manifests only after a safe lease/grace period. Keep the inactive-owner insertion fence. Acceptance: cancellation between upload and commit creates no downloadable packet; orphan cleanup eventually removes its bytes; accounts beyond10,000 entries finish erasure through bounded retries, without deleting another account or reopening spending. Dependencies: storage enumeration semantics, retention decision and support recovery path. Estimate: **2–4 days**. Cost effect: bounds accumulated storage and manual support effort. This is a synthetic scale failure, not evidence that any pilot account reached the threshold.

**BACKEND-03 — P2: question syntax does not establish factual grounding.** Source-confirmed and locally observed. `job-loop-providers.ts:95–104` validates question form, length, selected IDs and digits, but accepts unsupported roles and achievements. A mocked response attached “How did you lead the global merger team as managing director?” to a claim containing only a coursework report; `error` remained null. Runtime `259–263` then places these questions into the packet interview brief and downloadable evidence text. CV facts still come from approved literal claims, which limits the impact, but a student can receive a presupposition of senior employment. Use deterministic questions anchored to literal selected evidence, or require a model to choose a constrained question template and claim IDs. Acceptance: invented titles, employers, outcomes and attribution cannot enter questions; source quotations and claim revision references remain visible. Dependencies: preparation prompt contract and packet evidence renderer. Estimate: **1–2 days**. Cost effect: templates can reduce output tokens.
