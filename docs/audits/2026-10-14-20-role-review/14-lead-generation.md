## Findings

**LG-01 · P1 — A partner list does not cover the participant market.** The 20-row CSV contains coach businesses, while the acquisition plan’s first hypothesis is an experienced UAE strategy/transformation professional (`../jobvance-product-research/CUSTOMER-ACQUISITION-PLAN.md:9`). Its six participants come through two coaches (line 92). That can overrepresent coached professionals and miss student/MBA preparation needs. Prepare three separate channel hypotheses: opted-in university/project communities, MBA recruiting/alumni organizers, and existing professional relationships plus relevant coaches. Partners facilitate consented introductions; they are not counted as users. **Accept:** every planned participant has one segment, source and real task; ten target places provisionally split 3 student /3 MBA /4 professional. **Dependency:** founder’s actual relationships and organizer permission, currently unverified. **Effort:** 0.5–1 engineering days equivalent for a manual recruitment sheet; $0 tools.

**LG-02 · P1 — “High fit” currently means service relevance, not pilot readiness.** Sampled official sources support Dubai career services: [Linkk Search](https://www.linkksearch.com/) describes executive positioning and coaching, [Zeta Yarwood](https://zetayarwood.com/career-coaching/) describes senior-professional job search, and [Thrive Transitions](https://thrive-transitions.com/about-kinga-malec-career-transition-coach/) describes transitions. Retrieved 14 October; the Thrive result reports an older crawl, so recent operation is not established. None establishes a willing introduction, exact 5–10-year participant fit or recurring CV-review volume. Split `service_fit` from `pilot_ready`; record unknowns explicitly and recheck service/contact pages immediately before any later authorized approach. **Accept:** readiness requires an existing CV, actual vacancy within 30 days, available review time, voluntary feedback and permission to use their chosen material; partner readiness additionally requires relevant workflow and a consented introduction. **Dependency:** later discovery answers. **Effort:** 0.5–1 day; no enrichment purchase.

**LG-03 · P1 — Older screening would select for the concept demo instead of the shipped first task.** The plan requires two directions (lines 21,92); `/pilot` leads to a prewritten strategy/transformation demo and an unsent discussion draft (`PilotLanding.tsx:57–83,108–120`). **LG-04 · P1 — There is no complete acquisition-to-learning denominator.** `/pilot` deliberately records no received request (`pilot-request.ts:11–35`); admin explicitly says historical guest/check telemetry is incomplete (`AdminFounder.tsx:43–48`). ## Prepared first-ten playbook

1. Prepare a qualification card, the manual ledger and three short invitation drafts using the audience routes. No list expansion or sending in this audit. Plan 12–16 founder hours across intake, sessions, follow-up and notes; this is an assumption to measure.
2. Subject to later recruitment authorization, work in two small cohorts: first one participant per segment, then fill the provisional 3/3/4 mix using observed friction. Use existing relationships or organizer-approved invitations, never inferred job-seeker status. One unanswered follow-up maximum; stop immediately on decline.
3. In each session compare the participant’s usual method with one Jobvance task; include setup, corrections and founder assistance. Ask for optional week-two reuse on another real vacancy. Report free research participants, not paying customers.
4. Use planning gates, not statistical benchmarks: after ten qualified invitations with no substantive discussions, revise one channel/message variable; after three sessions with repeated task failure or no useful reviewed output, pause recruitment and fix it. At ten sessions, continue only if at least six complete a usable approved download and four voluntarily reuse it; inspect each segment separately. These are proposed decision rules, not observed results.

Do not build a scraper, enrichment pipeline, CRM integration, automated follow-up, coach dashboard or ad campaign now. No source establishes demand, conversion rate or willingness to pay.
