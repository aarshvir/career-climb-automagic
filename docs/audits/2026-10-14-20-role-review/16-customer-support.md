## Founder’s tier-one decision tree

1. **Collect safely:** page, step, exact error, time/timezone, browser/device, and non-secret run/packet reference. Never request full CV, password, receipt secret or card details. Confirm whether the issue is public/local or saved/private.
2. **Input blocked?** Unsupported/large/scanned file→`/help#uploads`; no CV→`#no-cv`. Have the candidate review converted/pasted text themselves. Repeatable failure with a small fictional supported file→engineering.
3. **Wording or approval blocked?** Compare vacancy/source/context; correct facts, save, rebuild, preview and approve. Do not “fix” evidence by inventing qualifications. Format failure→another ready format; persistent exact-PDF failure→engineering with sanitized reproduction.
4. **Sign-in or draft lost?** Use password reset/sign-in guidance. Establish tab/account, recovery choice and30-minute window; check saved versions. Expired memory-only text requires re-entry; do not promise restoration.
5. **Run incomplete or held?** Read actual lane/status/message. Not configured→manual vacancy/free-check fallback; partial→preserve usable outputs. Refresh before one explicit resume/cancel. Unknown paid outcome/cost hold→founder ledger review; never repeatedly retry, change consent/provider or increase the$50 cap to clear a ticket.
6. **Privacy?** Explain loop-only scope; account-wide/partial erasure→CS-02 owner checklist. Suspected cross-account exposure or destructive loss→immediate founder/engineering incident review; preserve minimal evidence and pause affected processing where authorized.

Check both inboxes once each working day as an internal founder practice, not a public response guarantee. Mark `in_review` after actual review; record the action/reply, candidate-visible next step and completion evidence before `resolved`. Prioritize CS-01/02, then CS-03/04. Learn from anonymized issue categories and successful recovery, not ticket volume alone. Do not build live chat, a fake human availability promise, a paid chatbot or separate support products for each persona now.
