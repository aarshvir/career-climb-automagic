# Jobvance improvement plan toward an evidenced 95+ experience

12 October 2026. Baseline: production version **12**, source `2e134180a8cdbb248c5fa5dca01b1a1bf8b1fb69`. This is a proposed work programme, not a claim that the work or its benefits have occurred. The [consolidated review](CONSOLIDATED-REVIEW.md) preserves the baseline; the [contract](../../ADMIN-AND-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-CONTRACT.md) controls delivery boundaries.

Prioritize a candidate finding suitable roles and preparing a second accurate application with less effort. A more polished interface alone will not establish those benefits. All acceptance thresholds below are **UNVALIDATED targets** until measured. The proposed $15/$29 prices are research stimuli only; checkout stays disabled. No purchases, subscriptions, advertisements, external outreach or automatic employer submissions are part of this plan. Preparation, factual sharing and submission stay under human control.

## Staged ten-person free pilot

Maximum **10 active pilot accounts**, with **4 students/new graduates, 3 MBA pivots, 3 employed professionals**. Count existing pilot occupancy first; any new enrollment must fit the remaining ten-slot allowance, and the cohort may include existing eligible consented pilot users. Never raise the cap to fill the research sample. The founder selects from existing consented volunteers; this plan does not authorize recruitment messages or advertising. Maintain a waitlist only with separate consent. Pilot participants may stop at any time; do not count replacing a dropout as the same person or conceal the original denominator. Use synthetic accounts for engineering gates and exclude them from human metrics. Keep participants' raw CVs in their authorized product storage; research notes use pseudonyms and redacted excerpts. Do not send participant records to external audit reviewers.

1. **Preparation, week 0:** lock source/deployment versions, consent script and tasks; verify ten-slot/$50 shared-cap controls; complete the engineering/control checks from phases 1–5 with synthetic negative cases; measure their human acceptance targets during the pilot. Agree target role families/locations with each volunteer and take a simple baseline of their usual preparation effort. Have a second reviewer label vacancies/evidence blind to algorithm output. Check instructions do not promise a job or assign an ATS/eligibility verdict.
2. **Wave A, days 1–3:** two students, one MBA and one professional complete a first/second application without coaching. Observe with their explicit permission; note help needed, misleading language, losses and reasons for stopping. They select a real public vacancy or a disclosed fictional fallback; report the fallback separately. Fix severe factual, privacy or approval failures before expanding; rerun affected tasks at the corrected version and preserve the original attempts.
3. **Wave B, days 4–7:** admit the remaining two students, two MBA and two professionals only after the gate. Each prepares two accurate packets and records one confirmed deadline/follow-up. Approved export is the endpoint; downloading and actual employer submission are different events. An application status changes only on the participant's own report. No operator sends applications or contacts on their behalf.
4. **Return test, days 8–14:** participants explicitly opt in to rules-only scheduling if ready. Observe browser-closed preparation, return/review, pause/cancel and one planned recovery scenario. Record unconfigured services or exhausted allowance as blocks, not successful runs. Do not require AI/search spend; deterministic preparation and confirmed/pasted employer sources remain usable. Reserve paid calls only with existing explicit candidate consent and cap clearance; no blind retry/fallback or new credit purchase.
5. **Repeat value, days 15–28:** aim for five participant-approved, useful packets each across independently relevant vacancies. Capture unsupervised voluntary returns, active minutes, errors, usefulness and unresolved gaps. Ask proposed-price/free/none choice after use, without rewards contingent on a positive response. Keep actual money collected and paid conversions at zero/unavailable as appropriate. Record alternatives such as university career services, generic tools and spreadsheets.
6. **Decision, day 29:** publish per-persona and overall denominators, first/second paired results, dropouts, assistance, spend and missing measures. Request a fresh independent review of the exact verified build and sanitized evidence packet. If coverage or repeat-use value fails, narrow the supported persona/market or improve that bottleneck before broadening. A ten-person convenience sample is formative, not a population estimate or proof of willingness to pay.

## Regression and independent re-review gates

- **Before human exposure:** required type/build checks and focused regression checks for stage/salary/location filtering, coursework/credential context, first→second state, exact export/approval binding, cap concurrency, idempotency, consent/revocation/isolation and erasure scope pass on the intended source. Run representative real browser/device journeys; record failures, versions and hashes. Tests validate these controls, not customer usefulness or hiring success.
- **After any correction:** rerun the affected scenario plus its closest negative control; recheck approval, data-sharing and export paths when revisions can affect them. Do not repeatedly broaden tests without a changed risk or failed check. Coursework/page-target and admin/router acceptance remain separate postbaseline records.
- **Before calling automation reliable:** show an actual opted-in browser-closed occurrence, visible truthful receipt, returning review handoff and pause/cancel; inject each listed failure once. Record attempt coverage separately from preparation completion. Human submission stays explicit and outside the scheduler.
- **Before saying time saved:** use consented human active intervals, comparable task boundaries and paired first/second observations; exclude automation/tool latency from human effort. Report full denominators, assistance, abandonment and unavailable results; no fixed claim based on code speed.
- **Before rerating:** pin revised source and verified deployed identity; freeze a sanitized evidence packet including failed checks and actual service configuration/coverage. Give fresh GPT 6.1 Sol, Kimi K3 and Muse Spark 1.3 reviewers the same ten dimensions and persona briefs without old scores, this target table or each other's reports. Clearly disclose native live-journey versus external packet evidence as in this baseline; retain requested/returned IDs, effort, packet hash, usage and raw outputs. Maximum provider spend and candidate consent remain enforced; missing models remain missing.
- **Before declaring 95+:** recompute ten-category, three-model and three-persona means independently. Publish min/max disagreement and unknowns even if the mean exceeds 95. Show human need/reuse evidence and label stated price preference accurately. If willingness to pay remains unverified under the free-beta boundary, report that limitation; never adjust weights, hide a low model score or invent customer/payment success to reach the target.
