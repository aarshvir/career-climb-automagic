Owner, lifecycle, request identity, lease and budget controls remain in force.

Schedules support one weekday, exactly three chosen weekdays, or every calendar day (including the 31st), using IANA timezones. Stable occurrence identity survives lease retry; missed time creates one occurrence rather than a backlog. Pause/cancel wins delayed admission. Recurring AI explicitly binds provider, model, route and the selected CV/facts. Existing rules schedules are not silently upgraded.

Private server-held subscription entitlement tables and routines enforce 1/3/7 weekly allowances, expiry/revocation and test/live separation. They are groundwork for recurring billing: existing Dodo one-time-pass handlers are retained; no recurring checkout/webhook product mapping is claimed. Merchant verification remains an external commerce limitation.
