Read the steps for this product tour. Timings are estimates for the written guide. A recording is not available for this selection.
Add your CV and the job
Planned narration 0:00–0:33 · Add your CV and the job
Upload your CV as a PDF, Word or text file up to five megabytes, or paste it into Your CV. Remove anything you would not share with an employer. Paste the full job ad into The job description. The job title is read from the ad, so check it, and add the company if you like. Select Tailor my CV for this job. This sends your CV and the job to our AI provider, and the AI may only reword what is already in your CV. To keep everything on this device, select Compare without AI instead.
Interactive step caption: PDF, Word or text, up to 5 MB. Scanned PDFs need readable text. No account needed.
Review changes
Planned narration 0:33–1:08 · What changed for this job · Line 9
Under What changed for this job, each suggested change shows the new wording beside your original line. Ask whether you could explain it in an interview. If not, select Keep my original. An added profile summary is written only from lines of your CV; select Remove this summary if you do not want it. Answer Quick questions only if the answer is true. Then read Your tailored CV, edit anything, and keep dates and numbers exact. If you compared without AI, this box is called Your CV for this job. Select Continue to download.
Interactive step caption: Each change shows the line of your own CV it came from. You decide what stays.
Download
Planned narration 1:08–1:42 · Download
Read your final CV. The PDF preview appears automatically and is the exact file you will download. Under Maximum PDF length, choose one or two pages. If the preview is longer, go back and shorten it, or use Word or text. If the panel is blank, open the exact preview in a new tab. When you have read every page, tick I have checked this CV. It is accurate and safe to share with employers. Downloads stay off until you do. Select Download PDF, Download Word or Download TXT. You send the application yourself.
Interactive step caption: The PDF preview is the exact file you download. The confirmation is yours, and you send the CV yourself.
