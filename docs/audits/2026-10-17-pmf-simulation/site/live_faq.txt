Questions about using Jobvance
What the check does, what stays private, and what happens next.
01 Can I check my CV without signing up?
Yes. Check your CV and download the version you approve, all for free. Sign in only if you want to save it privately. No purchase is required.
02 Does a finding mean I meet a requirement?
No. Matching words are a starting point. Read the requirement and the CV example yourself; the check cannot confirm your qualifications or predict what an employer will decide.
03 Does Jobvance apply for me?
You submit on the employer’s site. The tracker records the progress you report. Opening a link does not count as a submission.
04 Does Jobvance rewrite my CV?
It rewords and reorders what is already in your CV so it reads well for the job, and adds a short profile built only from your own lines. It never adds employers, numbers, tools or qualifications you have not listed, and it asks you about gaps instead of filling them. You check every change before downloading.
05 Can I download my documents?
Your approved CV can be downloaded as PDF, DOCX or TXT. Your original uploaded file can also be downloaded.
06 Which files can I upload?
Text-based PDF, DOCX and TXT files up to 5 MB. Scanned PDFs and old .doc files need conversion to a readable supported format.
07 What does Jobvance cost?
Free to start: 3 tailored CVs a month. Passes from $9, one-time. Signed in, you get 3 AI-tailored CVs each month; without an account, 1 a day. For more, a one-time pass gives you Sprint: 25 tailored CVs over 7 days for $9, Job Search: 100 over 30 days for $19, or Career Move: 300 over 90 days for $39. Passes are one-time payments in US dollars: no subscription, and nothing renews. Compare without AI, editing, downloads and the applications tracker stay free.
08 Are interviews or jobs guaranteed?
No. Jobvance helps prepare and organize applications; employer decisions and hiring outcomes remain outside its control.
09 Can I use projects or internships in my CV?
Yes. Your reviewed CV can include clearly labelled academic projects, internships, activities and previous employment. Describe your own contribution accurately. Project work is not automatically evidence of required professional experience.
10 How does the cloud Job Loop work?
Sign in, save your career record, confirm the facts you can share and add real vacancies. Prepare separate CVs and drafts. Review and approve each document before downloading. Search, model and inbox connections depend on operator setup; the workspace shows which are available. You send messages and submit applications yourself.
