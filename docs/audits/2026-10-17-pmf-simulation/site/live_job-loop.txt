Before you start
Do I need to complete a long profile?
Start with a CV or use guided questions to build one. Review your experience, choose target roles and locations, then save your setup. CV Check is a separate way to try one application without an account.
Will every run find new jobs?
No. A run can find no suitable new vacancies, and a source can be unavailable. The dashboard shows the actual result and unfinished steps. You can add a vacancy yourself and prepare it directly.
What does Job Loop include?
The cloud pilot has ten places and a shared processing allowance. It includes selected employer feeds, rather than the whole job market. Broad web search and inbox access are not currently connected.
Optional rules-based schedules can prepare documents while your browser is closed. Configured external models require separate consent for each run. Your workspace shows what ran and the result, and you can pause or cancel a schedule.
Checkout is disabled, so there is no card requirement or automatic conversion to a paid plan. Read the current free offer .
Will Jobvance send an application for me?
You approve the document and apply on the employer’s site yourself. No application or message is sent automatically, and opening a vacancy does not mark it as applied.
What if I do not have a CV yet?
Use the guided CV builder in Job Loop. Answer questions about your education, work and projects, then review the draft made from your own answers. Follow the no-CV guide or read the career guides .
Where can I find help with a step?
Walk through the first application or find a specific answer . The setup steps also link to relevant guides. Once you have saved a private record, you can return to it later.
