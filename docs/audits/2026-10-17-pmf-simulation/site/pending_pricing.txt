Questions about pricing
What counts as a tailored CV?
Each time you choose “Tailor my CV for this job” and the AI prepares a version, that uses one. If the request fails before any AI work is done, it does not count. Comparing without AI, editing and downloading never use one.
What stays free?
Compare without AI, editing, PDF, Word and text downloads, and the applications tracker stay free with no limit. Signed in, you also get 3 AI-tailored CVs every calendar month (UTC). Without an account you get 1 a day. Job Loop is free during its beta.
Do passes include Job Loop runs?
No. Passes add AI-tailored CVs for the CV check. Job Loop is free during its beta; its monthly plans are proposals and cannot be bought yet.
Can I buy another pass while one is active?
Yes. Its tailored CVs are added to what you have, and its days start when your current pass ends, so nothing is wasted. The pass that ends soonest is used first. For fair use, any account can tailor up to 40 CVs a day.
What if a pass isn’t right for me?
If you have used fewer than 5 tailored CVs, ask within 7 days of buying and we will refund you in full. A refunded pass stops working, including any CVs left on it. Read the refund policy .
Who will my invoice come from?
Dodo Payments sells passes for us as merchant of record. They take the payment, handle sales tax and email your invoice, and their name may appear on your card statement.
