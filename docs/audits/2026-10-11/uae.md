# UAE bilingual career-switcher audit — 11 October 2026

This is a simulated persona review, not a customer interview. The persona is an employed UAE resident preparing an English application for a different role, using English and Arabic professionally. They want to record Dubai/Abu Dhabi preferences, an explicit AED monthly pay requirement, employer-specific work-authorization questions and their own notice period, while keeping current employment confidential. No salary range, immigration entitlement, employer requirement or legal rule is assumed.

**Conclusion:** Jobvance presents a credible, controlled English preparation workflow. Its UAE usefulness is constrained by late document-language limits, generic unresolved eligibility/pay questions, and an unavailable support contact. The current product should earn trust through clear boundaries and a useful manual workflow before selling discovery or bilingual output.

## What works today

**LIVE:** Career-switchers receive relevant guidance about transferable experience, supporting versus leading work, and preparing around employment. The fictional illustration changes to operations analysis when selected. The six-step explanation is understandable, and the mobile audience buttons, main CTA and navigation are legible and responsive. Public pages consistently say that users review their words, submit through employers themselves, and can start free without a card.

**LIVE:** Privacy distinguishes device analysis, optional account saving, external model consent and separate inbox permission. It says downloaded documents cannot be recalled. Pricing distinguishes a free beta from a disabled planned pass. These are meaningful controls for someone still employed.

**SOURCE:** The career record accepts free-form locations and languages, explicit compensation currency/period/minimum, a discreet-search preference and excluded terms ([JobLoop.tsx:269](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/JobLoop.tsx#L269>), [270](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/JobLoop.tsx#L270>)). AED/monthly is representable; the engine requires an explicit three-letter currency. It preserves employment context, confirmed shareable claims, factual approval and separate evidence. Tracking requires users to attest status changes rather than interpreting preparation as application submission ([JobLoop.tsx:83](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/JobLoop.tsx#L83>)). These capabilities remain unexecuted in this audit.

## Nine improvements with acceptance criteria

**1. P1 — Explain the exact English/Arabic and glyph boundary before input.** The public introduction offers PDF/Word/text without a visible language qualification. The check later says English wording analysis. Arabic U+0600–U+06FF triggers manual review rather than a match ([cv-evidence.ts:56](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/cv-evidence.ts#L56>), [302](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/cv-evidence.ts#L302>), [355](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/cv-evidence.ts#L355>); SOURCE, [check route](https://jobvance.io/check)). Read-only font-table inspection found no mapped glyph for the characters in `محمد العربية`; the frontend and embedded cloud font bytes are identical. Free PDF export names unsupported characters and offers DOCX/TXT ([document-export.ts:65](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/document-export.ts#L65>)). Cloud PDF failure holds the entire packet, even though DOCX/TXT artifacts can be created ([job-loop-render.ts:33](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-render.ts#L33>), [48](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-render.ts#L48>), [58](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-render.ts#L58>)). **Accept:** before analysis/save, identify English-only analysis and preflight Arabic names/text; show affected characters and safe supported next steps. Preserve Unicode without inventing translation. Advertise Arabic PDF only after name preservation, shaping, direction and copy/paste are verified. This is a SOURCE limitation, not a live failed download.

**2. P2 — Turn eligibility and notice-period uncertainty into a per-vacancy checklist.** The engine deliberately returns `ELIGIBILITY_NOT_VERIFIED` for every assessment; the strict profile whitelist has no work-authorization or notice-period field ([job-loop-engine.ts:42](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-engine.ts#L42>), [208](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-engine.ts#L208>); SOURCE, [loop route](https://jobvance.io/loop)). Generic notes can hold this information, but do not guide a working resident. **Accept:** optional user-attested availability/notice information and explicit vacancy questions, each with source, unknown/manual-review state and sharing consent. Never infer visa status, sponsorship, employer acceptance or legal eligibility. Keep private identifiers unnecessary.

**3. P2 — Make compensation preferences actionable without estimating pay.** Currency/monthly/minimum can be entered, but every assessment returns `PAY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`; there is no vacancy pay field or actual comparison with the saved minimum (same engine:208; JobLoop:270). **Accept:** display “AED per month” unambiguously when selected; capture employer-stated amount, period and source separately; distinguish unknown from below preference. Do not convert undisclosed pay, infer ranges or present a fit score as salary validation.

**4. P2 — Make UAE location matching more precise.** Location currently contributes points through case-insensitive substring matching ([job-loop-engine.ts:210](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-engine.ts#L210>)). That cannot establish country, emirate, commute feasibility, remote eligibility or relocation terms. **Accept:** keep original location wording and separate verified country/city/work arrangement from user preferences. Unknown or ambiguous remote coverage needs review. Explain location points without implying a UAE hiring authorization.

**5. P2 — Clarify and strengthen discreet-search controls.** The existing toggle is explicitly a review reminder, not an automatic contact block or confidentiality guarantee ([job-loop-engine.ts:273](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-engine.ts#L273>)); the UI’s shorter toggle text may be read more strongly. Excluded terms are real rendering holds ([job-loop-render.ts:18](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-render.ts#L18>)). **Accept:** explain the reminder beside the toggle, preview current-employer details in all outputs, and allow an explicit employer/contact exclusion list. Show what is blocked versus merely flagged; require review of any override. Do not promise anonymity or erase employment chronology silently.

**6. P2 — Expose current discovery availability before the account gate.** LIVE home leads with Replies and Search & shortlist; a later qualifier says setup is needed. RELEASE reused confirms these connections are currently unavailable. Runtime truthfully records blocked LinkedIn sources and unavailable search ([job-loop-runtime.ts:130](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-runtime.ts#L130>), [143](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-runtime.ts#L143>)). **Accept:** public Loop description states the presently usable manual vacancy/rules workflow and labels unavailable discovery/inbox lanes. Enable sources only after configured permitted access is verified; do not suggest a UAE-wide search ran.

**7. P1 — Give privacy requests a usable contact route.** LIVE [privacy](https://jobvance.io/privacy) tells users to contact support for account erasure, but LIVE [contact](https://jobvance.io/contact) says direct contact details are unavailable ([ProductPage.tsx:160](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/ProductPage.tsx#L160>), [194](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/ProductPage.tsx#L194>)). **Accept:** publish and verify a monitored support/request channel reachable without signing in; explain required minimal identifiers and confirmation. Clarify deletion across originals, checks, claims, packs and downloaded copies. A user must be able to submit an erasure request without sending a full CV.

**8. P2 — Treat a follow-up date as a calendar day.** SOURCE saves the selected date at 12:00 UTC, while Today requires its instant to have passed ([JobLoop.tsx:83](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/JobLoop.tsx#L83>), [248](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/JobLoop.tsx#L248>)). **INFERENCE:** in Dubai a date-only follow-up enters the due list at 16:00, missing a morning review. **Accept:** store date-only intent with an explicit user timezone, or ask for a time. A selected Dubai day is due during that morning, remains stable after reload/travel, and displays its timezone when time matters. This was not executed live.

**9. P2 — Define the paid value and currency before offering it.** LIVE [pricing](https://jobvance.io/pricing) shows a planned `$29` ten-pack pass alongside free downloads and optional saving. The beta, limited connections and free Loop make the incremental benefit unclear; currency is not written as an ISO code. **Accept:** keep purchase disabled until support works and the paid entitlement is demonstrably useful. State its currency explicitly, distinguish it from compensation currency, describe included capabilities and limits, and validate repeat-use value with users. Do not invent AED pricing or an exchange-rate guarantee.

## Top three and use/pay hypotheses

Prioritize **1: early language/glyph preflight**, **7: accessible privacy support**, then **2: manual eligibility/availability checklist**. These resolve concrete trust and usability barriers while preserving the honest English workflow.

**INFERENCE, not customer evidence:** this persona may use the free check for English vacancies because it surfaces source passages and does not contact their employer. Repeat use may come from reusable shareable facts and a reliable morning follow-up list. Willingness to pay remains unproven: the hypothesis is reduced preparation effort across several relevant vacancies, with reliable formats and clear privacy control. Evaluate whether users complete a second application and can explain every changed claim before measuring paid conversion. Neither current test counts nor the fictional demo establishes demand, interviews or job outcomes.
