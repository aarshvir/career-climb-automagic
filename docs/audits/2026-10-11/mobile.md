# Independent mobile applicant audit — Jobvance, 11 October 2026

Jobvance is a credible free, careful CV wording check, but it is not yet a dependable mobile job-search companion. Its strongest experience is the explicit approval boundary; its largest mobile risk is losing a complete draft while reading the product’s own privacy page. The immediate opportunity is less repetitive reading, safer interruption recovery and clearer evidence of what the ongoing service currently delivers.

This is a **simulated customer-expert assessment, not an interview**. Persona: a student or career-switcher using a phone between other commitments, sometimes with weak connectivity and a keyboard or accessibility need. I independently inspected the public site, a fictional signed-out guest check, relevant source and the supplied release status. I did not read other reviewers’ findings. No account/product records, personal files, messages, applications or payments were created. Viewports 390×844 and 320×740 were restored. [Live trace](<mobile-liveevidence/trace.md>) records the fixture, measurements and reproduction steps.

**Evidence labels:** LIVE = browser observation; SOURCE = inspected implementation; RELEASE reused = supplied release evidence, not independently rerun; INFERENCE = product judgment; Not verified = untested. Confidence is stated per finding. Private Loop execution and real phone behavior remain unknown. The release’s 410 tests are useful engineering evidence, not proof of usability or WCAG conformance. No network interruption, assistive-technology, contrast or text-zoom audit was performed.

## Patterns worth learning from six current official sources

These are documented product patterns, not proof of competitors’ advertised results or of their unseen private apps. Do not copy their branding or adopt interview multipliers, ATS-success claims or ratings as truth.

- **Teal:** its [resume-building guide](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/14435724-how-to-build-your-resume-in-teal) explains one comprehensive source record, per-role bullet selection and user-controlled syncing. Jobvance should make “reuse a fact, choose it for this role, review any revision” understandable without re-editing a long text blob.
- **Huntr:** its [Job Card guide](https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/12640406-understanding-the-job-card) brings source URL, deadline, application log, interview details and documents into one opportunity record. Learn the continuity of a role’s history and visible next date, rather than adding more disconnected screens.
- **Simplify:** its [Copilot guide](https://help.simplify.jobs/help/articles/2415391-using-copilot-to-autofill-applications) describes context beside the employer application, review before manual submission and a manual-copy fallback. Jobvance should reduce repeat entry and explain the external handoff; automatic submission is unnecessary to learn that pattern.
- **Jobscan:** its [resume scanner page](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-scanner) documents separate keyword, formatting and content feedback with an edit/rescan loop. Learn specific, categorized actions; preserve Jobvance’s stronger caution that wording evidence does not establish eligibility.
- **Rezi:** its [download guide](https://intercom.help/rezihelp/en/articles/8385104-download-your-resume) explicitly explains preview, format compatibility and editable DOCX export. Learn format-specific guidance and a clear preview-to-file relationship, without copying paid limits.
- **Kickresume:** its [mobile product page](https://www.kickresume.com/en/mobile-app/) describes section editing and continuation across phone and desktop. Learn reliable resumption and manageable editing chunks; the page alone does not verify app quality or security claims.

## Nine current gaps and reviewable acceptance criteria

**1. P1 — Internal navigation silently destroys a reviewed draft. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence.** At step 6 I followed the built-in Privacy link, then Back: the check returned to blank step 1. The warning mentions refresh, while internal navigation also unmounts state. This undermines the applicant who checks disclosure terms before approval. See [CvCheck.tsx:85](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L85>), [CvCheck.tsx:115](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L115>), and [ProductPage.tsx:156](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/ProductPage.tsx#L156>). **Accept:** privacy/help can be read without discarding the active check; every destructive departure offers a clear choice. Any optional tab retention must be explicit, scoped, time-limited and clearable. Test Back, menu links and refresh separately, preserving the same reviewed content or obtaining an explicit discard choice.

**2. P2 — The evidence step demands excessive mobile scrolling. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence.** My short vacancy produced eight cards and a 6,640px page at 390px; the next action sat at y=6,234. Repeated caveats and generic questions compete with the useful source quotes. “Focus on review questions” does not prioritize this fixture because all eight findings need review. [CvCheck.tsx:61](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L61>), [CvCheck.tsx:244](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L244>). **Accept:** show an actionable summary, severity/category filter and compact requirement list first; expand full evidence on demand. Keep a reachable next-step action and preserve reading position. An eight-finding fixture should permit understanding priorities and moving to review without traversing every expanded card.

**3. P2 — Vacancy intake gives limited help with mobile copy/paste. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence.** URL-only paste receives a generic character-count error. There is no optional source-link record in the guest form. The role/employer title became a “requirement,” adding noise to review. [CvCheck.tsx:243](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L243>), [cv-evidence.ts:143](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/cv-evidence.ts#L143>). **Accept:** detect URL-only input and explain that posting text is required; retain an optional manually supplied source URL without implying it was fetched or verified. Let applicants inspect extracted requirements and exclude obvious metadata while keeping the complete vacancy available. Preserve pasted text when correcting an input error.

**4. P2 — Invalid-input recovery lacks field-level guidance and focus. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence.** For a 27-character URL the alert appeared, but focus remained on “Check these inputs.” The textarea had no observed invalid state or error association. An applicant must find the problem and navigate back manually. [CvCheck.tsx:237](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L237>), [check-draft.ts:10](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/check-draft.ts#L10>). **Accept:** associate the message with the field, mark invalid input, move focus to the relevant field/error summary and keep all valid content. Test keyboard and screen-reader feedback for role, CV and vacancy errors; avoid merely adding more alert text.

**5. P2 — Mobile reading comfort is uneven. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence for measurements, medium for impact.** Primary controls measured 44px high and textareas use 16px: good. However, the six-column rail uses 9px labels; disclosure summaries measured 32px high, with abundant small captions. At 320px the rail columns were 46px wide. This is a comfort concern, not a WCAG violation assertion. [CvCheck.css:74](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.css#L74>), [CvCheck.css:41](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.css#L41>). **Accept:** use legible progress labels, larger disclosure targets and an explicit current-step label. Verify 320px, text enlargement and keyboard focus without truncation or page-level horizontal scrolling. Reduce repeated above-form guidance after the first step.

**6. P2 — Reviewing changes is a whole-document memory task. SOURCE + INFERENCE; medium/high confidence.** The current review offers one large textarea and says bullets may move. It does not show a dedicated original-versus-current change list. An applicant on a phone must remember what changed, then attest to the whole result. [CvCheck.tsx:245](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/CvCheck.tsx#L245>), [cv-evidence.ts:363](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/lib/cv-evidence.ts#L363>). **Accept:** show additions, removals and moved passages by section, with source context and a simple undo. Keep whole-CV reading and exact approval mandatory. Demonstrate an actual reordered experience block and a user-added fact; unchanged text should not generate a spurious change notice.

**8. P1 — There is no usable public support/recovery route. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence.** Contact explicitly says direct support details are unavailable. Help repeats the six steps but offers no troubleshooting for paste, scanned PDFs, interrupted downloads or lost sessions. This prevents a stuck applicant recovering independently and is material to payment readiness. [ProductPage.tsx:191](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/ProductPage.tsx#L191>), [ProductPage.tsx:194](<https://github.com/aarshvir/career-climb-automagic/blob/2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414/src/pages/ProductPage.tsx#L194>). **Accept:** provide working support contact and specific recovery guidance reachable from errors without draft loss. **9. P3 — Escape does not close mobile navigation from its toggle. LIVE + SOURCE; high confidence.** Open menu, focus still on “Close navigation,” press Escape: remains open. Preserve normal Tab navigation and visible focus.

## Priority and use/pay hypotheses

Top three: **protect drafts when reading privacy/help**, **make evidence priorities and the next action reachable on a phone**, and **establish a usable recovery/support route**. Preserve the existing two approvals and approval invalidation after edits: these worked live. The keyboard sequence also reached the scrollable final preview; that was a positive observation, not an accessibility certification.

**INFERENCE, medium confidence:** I would use the free check for one vacancy when I already have readable CV text and time to inspect quotations. I would be less likely to return after one silent draft loss. I would not yet pay the planned $29 pass for mobile repeat use without reliable resumption, clear document changes and working support. A stronger paid hypothesis is saved, source-controlled career facts plus multiple role records and visible next actions. Validate it with observed repeat preparation and user-controlled resumption before assuming a demand for more AI or automatic applications.
