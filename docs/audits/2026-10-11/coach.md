# Independent coach audit — Jobvance, 11 October 2026

This is a **simulated UAE career coach/university careers adviser perspective**, not a customer interview, participant study or professional endorsement. I would recommend the current free CV check for a small, supervised preparation pilot. I would make account-based participation conditional on a clear support/data-request route and explain that live discovery and inbox services are presently unavailable according to the release record. The useful proposition is truthful preparation around one vacancy, with a candidate making the final decision.

Confidence is stated with findings and scores.

The repository base for file:line citations is `the repository root`. I read the specified routes, engine and release record without consulting other audit reports. Chrome’s public header indicated an existing session, so I avoided account routes there and used an unauthenticated in-app browser. No existing CV, tracker, account record or mailbox was opened. No account was created; no pilot request, application or message was submitted. My only working example was explicitly fictional and remained in the guest check. ## Adviser scenario: demo → review → pack → feedback → progress

**Public demo. LIVE, high confidence.** I inspected the prewritten Strategy example, opened all three source passages, kept the safe replacement, checked three facts and approved the fictional CV. Approval unlocked its download control. The rejected edit explained why “helped design” cannot become ownership and why projected savings cannot become delivered savings. A restricted fictional financial note was visibly excluded. This is a good five-minute discussion exercise for MBA candidates and employed switchers. It teaches claim discipline without needing anyone’s real CV. Evidence: `coach-live-pilot.txt`, `coach-live-demo-approved.txt`; `src/pages/PilotDemo.tsx:131`, `src/lib/pilot-demo.ts:25`.

**Student review. A fictional UAE vacancy also requested leadership, two years of professional experience, Arabic/English communication and work authorization. The interface found direct Python/Excel wording, treated assisted leadership and duration as related passages, and left language/authorization for manual review. It did not infer authorization from “Dubai, UAE” or declare the student qualified. Nine requirements were counted, including the title and fictional preamble, although I supplied seven substantive requirements. Evidence: `coach-live-student-findings.txt`; `src/pages/CvCheck.tsx:39`, `src/lib/cv-evidence.ts:143`.

**Source-grounded pack. LIVE/SOURCE/RELEASE reused, high for guest report, medium for cloud.** I rechecked the unchanged fictional CV, completed the two factual/privacy confirmations and approved it. The separate TXT evidence export contained vacancy quotes, CV quotes, line numbers, method, unresolved questions and explicit warnings. Its separation from the employer-facing CV is useful. I did not independently download/render guest PDF or DOCX, or run the authenticated cloud loop. The release record reports per-role CV/evidence/letter generation, immutable manifest hashes, private downloads and withdrawal checks with fictional accounts (`docs/CLOUD-JOB-LOOP-RELEASE-STATUS.md:7`, `:9`, `:18`). **Share feedback. SOURCE/INFERENCE, medium confidence.** A participant can choose to send their downloaded CV and evidence report to an adviser using their own channel. I would ask for the vacancy and exact version as well, then return three concrete questions about contribution, missing evidence and role suitability. I did not send anything. A shared coach account, institution portal, permissions dashboard or in-product comments are not promised; their absence is not a defect in this pilot. **Progress. SOURCE/RELEASE reused, medium confidence.** The loop supports saved/prepared/applied/interview/offer/rejected/withdrawn status, notes and follow-up dates. ## What earns a recommendation

The audiences are explicit and distinct: home guidance recognises coursework, MBA team projects and transferable work without granting new qualifications (`src/pages/ProductPage.tsx:49`). The free check delivers real utility before registration, with readable progress, back navigation and a final full-CV review (**LIVE, high**). The “related passage” treatment is stronger than a reassuring percentage score for a novice candidate. The source also distinguishes skills lists from examples, unresolved duration from established experience, and source text from candidate edits (`src/pages/CvCheck.tsx:47`, `:69`).

Controls are unusually relevant to an employed switcher: disclosed local analysis, optional account storage, per-claim sharing, restrictions, separate provider consent and no automatic submission. Cloud claim validation preserves complete literal passages, contribution, attribution and academic/employment context (`supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-engine.ts:84`). Paid models cannot author arbitrary new CV facts according to the release record (`docs/CLOUD-JOB-LOOP-RELEASE-STATUS.md:30`). **SOURCE/RELEASE reused, medium-high**; I did not audit every security implementation. The 390×844 final review remained legible and stacked vertically, with clear consent boxes (**LIVE, medium**, `coach-live-mobile-approved.png`).

## Precise gaps and acceptance criteria

1. **High — support/data-request route is inconsistent. LIVE/SOURCE, high confidence.** Privacy tells users to contact support for account erasure, but Contact states direct support details are unavailable. The pilot separately publishes `contact@jobvance.io`; it is not identified as the data-request route or verified as responsive. Evidence: `coach-live-contact.txt`, `coach-live-privacy.txt`; `src/pages/ProductPage.tsx:160`, `:194`. **Accept:** publish one clearly owned route across these pages, its purpose and expected response window; demonstrate a fictional access/erasure enquiry can be acknowledged. No need for an institution contract to solve this basic issue.

2. **Medium — requirement counts include non-requirements. LIVE/SOURCE, high.** The nine-row result included my vacancy title and preamble. The parser starts in an included section and promotes ordinary lines into clauses (`src/lib/cv-evidence.ts:146`, `:185`). **Accept:** title/company/preamble do not count as qualification findings in representative postings; preserve them separately and allow the user to review which clauses count. My fixture should show seven substantive requirements.

3. **Medium — mandatory-condition prompts lack specificity. LIVE/SOURCE, high.** Arabic/English communication and UAE authorization were safely flagged for manual review, but both received the generic “Is there something your CV should show here?” question. That can encourage filling a CV gap when the useful action is to clarify eligibility or recognise an unmet condition. The cloud engine also always flags eligibility/pay as unverified (`supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-engine.ts:208`). **Accept:** present condition-specific questions and distinguish “ask the employer/check reliable evidence” from “add a truthful experience example”; never infer rights or language proficiency from location. This is preparation guidance, not a request for automated legal advice.

4. **Medium — practical discovery availability appears late. SOURCE/RELEASE reused/INFERENCE, medium-high.** The home describes finding roles and reading replies with setup caveats, while the release record says search/model/mail capabilities reported unavailable and Gmail is disconnected (`docs/CLOUD-JOB-LOOP-RELEASE-STATUS.md:20`, `:32`). Guest `/loop` leads to sign-in. **Accept:** a public, current capability summary explains what this pilot can run now and that candidates should bring a vacancy; a configured discovery lane later demonstrates an actual employer URL, checked time and partial coverage. Lack of scraping or automatic applications is not a gap.

5. **Medium — first cloud record requires considerable interpretation. SOURCE/INFERENCE, medium.** Users save a reviewed source, suggest passages, select evidence context and supply context/wording/result status plus two confirmations per claim (`src/pages/JobLoop.tsx:69`, `:268`, `:273`). These controls are justified, but a beginner’s first packet may become a data-entry session. **Accept:** guide one claim and one vacancy before exposing the full library; observe consenting novices complete it without adviser transcription, documenting completion time and confusing fields. Actual completion time is **Not verified**.

6. **Medium — guest feedback handoff lacks a durable identity. LIVE/SOURCE, high.** My evidence export names the role and method but omits company, timestamp, source URL/full vacancy and an exact CV-version identifier (`src/lib/free-check-export.ts:27`). A coach receiving several attachments must reconcile them manually. Cloud packets have stronger version controls, so this criticism concerns the guest export. **Accept:** an optional local review bundle contains the approved CV, full vacancy and evidence, with matching version IDs/date and candidate-controlled disclosure. Coach login or online collaboration is unnecessary.

7. **Low — pilot interaction is less representative for first-job students. LIVE/SOURCE, high.** Its two interactive directions are Strategy Manager and Transformation Manager (`src/lib/pilot-demo.ts:69`, `:82`). Home student examples help, but the deeper demo still uses consulting work rather than coursework. **Accept:** add a student project/internship direction preserving academic labels and an unmet professional-experience condition; retain the current MBA/switcher example.

8. **Low — interrupted guest work has a recovery cost. LIVE/SOURCE/INFERENCE, high for behaviour, medium for impact.** Unsaved work clears on refresh; consented temporary retention is specific to Google sign-in (`src/lib/check-draft.ts:25`). This privacy choice is honestly disclosed, but busy students can lose a long review. **Accept:** warn on leaving a populated check or offer explicitly chosen, bounded tab retention and a clear erase control. Default silent persistence is unnecessary.

9. **Low — mobile complete-review assurance needs more testing. LIVE, medium.** At 390×844, the final CV is a nested scrolling panel above a long page. It is usable in the observed view, but completing a whole document read may be awkward. **Accept:** offer an expanded full-document view and verify keyboard focus, zoom and screen-reader flow. Those assistive-technology checks are **Not verified**.

**Top three:** resolve the support/data-request path; correct requirement counting and mandatory-condition prompts; make presently available cloud capabilities clear before registration. These reduce trust and decision friction within the existing product.

For a free supervised pilot I would begin with consenting participants, one real vacancy each, redacted source material and a clear agreement about what the adviser sees. Guest-only use can start now. Before account-based recommendation, resolve the support route and confirm current capabilities with the participant. Success criteria: the candidate can explain every claim; the pack preserves contribution/context; mandatory gaps remain visible; adviser feedback maps to the same version; and the candidate can name the next action. Repeating the process for a second role should require less reconstruction without reducing care. Employment results are outside this preparation test.

**Use hypotheses, INFERENCE, medium-low:** students may value guided evidence questions; MBAs may value reusable, distinct academic/work records; employed switchers may value disclosure control and follow-up visibility. **Pay hypothesis, INFERENCE, low:** some may pay for repeated high-quality packs if repeat preparation effort demonstrably falls. Live pricing lists a planned $29/30-day ten-pack pass with sales disabled (`src/pages/ProductPage.tsx:190`); it establishes no demand. A university recommendation does not establish an institutional buyer. I would first measure second-task completion, correction burden, useful output and voluntary return, then ask participants about concrete paid choices. No willingness-to-pay, interview improvement or customer outcome is claimed.
