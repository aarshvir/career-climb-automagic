# Jobvance independent customer-perspective audit

11 October 2026 · Current live version 10 · Source `2d1ad90356a7c952740dde2c9bf02ab5880a1414`

**Jobvance is not yet best in class. Consolidated readiness is 60.9/100 (rounded: 61/100). Your three core audiences average 56.8/100 (rounded: 57/100).**

The platform provides a useful, free, source-conscious application-preparation foundation. The as-is product still has concrete evidence/guidance defects, avoidable draft loss, unavailable discovery/inbox lanes, and incomplete repeat-use and support journeys. Visual polish is credible; the next priorities concern task completion, evidence precision and sustained usefulness.

These are six separately prompted **simulated customer-expert reviews**, run in two parallel batches of three. They are not customer interviews or external certifications. Reviewers did not see one another's reports. Weights are shared; judgments differ. The existing 410 tests/40 hosted checks are reused release evidence, not new customer tests or proof of satisfaction. None of the reviewers independently ran an authenticated cloud journey in this audit. Provider funding, actual hiring outcomes, support fulfillment, retention and willingness to pay remain unverified.

The current Site read confirmed active version 10 and no Site schedules. Source was unchanged during this audit. Model/search/mail disablement is documented in the same-day release receipt; it was not independently changed or activated.

## Concrete defects and immediate priorities

1. **Evidence scoping:** deleting only Python from a separate skills list changes an unchanged completed project's classification from review-required to direct wording. The student observed this live; the coordinator independently reproduced the pure function.
2. **Condition guidance:** a sponsorship restriction is paired with a sentence about not leading a team; graduation/closing dates receive budget/team-size guidance. These are manual-review findings, not false qualification certificates, but their guidance is unhelpful.
3. **Draft loss:** student and mobile reviewers independently lost the completed guest draft after in-app navigation. The mobile reviewer reproduced step six → Privacy → Back returning to an empty first step.
4. **Support/erasure:** live Contact supplies no usable support destination, while Privacy directs erasure there. A pilot address is not proof that the route is monitored or fulfills requests.
5. **Current service envelope:** user-supplied-vacancy preparation is available according to release evidence, but external discovery, inbox and daily scheduling do not deliver a complete recurring delegate today.
6. **Output confidence:** approvals show text instead of the actual page layout; one/two-page cloud holds demand another record-edit/rebuild cycle. Arabic glyph support is a source-verified limitation, including names inside an otherwise English CV.

## What should be preserved

- A useful guest check without a card or account, with explicit optional cloud saving.
- Visible source passages, academic/internship/employment distinctions and projected-versus-measured outcomes.
- Separate factual and disclosure approval; edits invalidate approval. Live guest controls behaved correctly in the coach/mobile reviews.
- Private source-grounded packets and honest manual tracking, supported by the previous hosted verification. These private claims were not independently executed in this audit.
- Honest unavailable/partial lanes and user-controlled sending/application submission.
- A coherent visual identity and readable public mobile layout. No WCAG, real-device performance or comparative design certification is claimed.

## Ordered implementation plan

**First: repair trust and prevent lost effort.** Start with F01/F02 evidence precision, F03 support, F04 capability clarity and F08 navigation recovery. These improve the existing product without adding providers or increasing API spend. Have an evidence engineer implement the parser corrections, a frontend engineer protect the draft, and the founder verify the support mailbox/process. Do not add testimonials or outcome metrics without real evidence.

**Next: make one complete repeat journey.** Deliver F05 permitted discovery, F06 guided record, F07 continuity, F09 exact document preview and F12 deadlines. Work toward: one source record → one sourced vacancy → defensible document → exact review → user application → one linked timeline. Operator credentials and source permissions are external dependencies. Retain the US$50 shared usage ceiling; use rules mode and permitted free feeds where appropriate.

**Then: specialize and validate repetition.** Prioritize F10 Arabic-name rendering, F11 actionable discretion, F13 explicit opt-in repetition, F21 mobile reading, and the remaining segment-specific improvements. Scheduling and Gmail must not be advertised as available until setup, permissions and actual operation are verified. The owner's free-beta/no-ads/no-outreach constraints continue to apply.

Effort ranges below are planning estimates for a competent engineer working in the existing codebase, not commitments or additive project totals. Integration/account approval time, production hardening and observed user feedback can change them. No account purchase, provider enabling, deployment, ad or outreach was performed for this audit.

## Six benchmark patterns

These current official sources describe expectations worth learning. They do not prove their own advertised outcome rates, establish a market ranking, or verify authenticated competitor behavior. The mobile reviewer researched these independently.

- [Teal](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/14435724-how-to-build-your-resume-in-teal): One comprehensive source record with per-role bullet selection and controlled syncing.
- [Huntr](https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/12640406-understanding-the-job-card): One opportunity brings together the source, deadlines, history, interviews and documents.
- [Simplify](https://help.simplify.jobs/help/articles/2415391-using-copilot-to-autofill-applications): Reduce repeated application entry, with review and a manual-copy fallback.
- [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-scanner): Categorized feedback and an edit/recheck loop; do not adopt unverifiable ATS or interview claims.
- [Rezi](https://intercom.help/rezihelp/en/articles/8385104-download-your-resume): A clear preview-to-download relationship and format-specific editable DOCX guidance.
- [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com/en/mobile-app/): Manageable section editing and continuation across phone and desktop.

## What would justify a stronger claim

Treat “best in class” as unproven until observed customers complete meaningful tasks reliably and return. Proposed next-pilot gates, **not achieved results**:

- Close reproduced evidence, draft-loss and support defects; test completed/pending skills, dates, negation, ownership and mixed scripts without invented qualifications.
- Observe at least ten consenting target users across all three audiences, including phone use, completing a real vacancy-to-approved-document task without developer intervention. Record denominators, assistance, elapsed time and reasons for abandonment.
- Verify first and second application preparation with the same record, exact preview/export equivalence, useful permitted source freshness, and transparent unavailable states. Measure net repeat-use effort rather than assuming AI saves time.
- Complete a fresh authenticated end-to-end audit with fictional accounts, including account-switch isolation, changed/withdrawn claims after interview, interrupted runs and format recovery. Test any enabled provider with bounded real usage before describing it as working.
- Verify actual erasure fulfillment and recovery/restore procedures. Source controls are not a substitute for tested operations.
- Ask users what they would continue using and why. Actual retention and willingness to pay require evidence; the planned price is not customer validation.

These gates can support a stronger readiness score, not guarantee employment, commercial success or comparative market leadership.
