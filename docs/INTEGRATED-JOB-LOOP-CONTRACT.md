# Integrated Job Loop release contract

11 October 2026, Asia/Dubai. Scope authorized by the owner: B1–B7, operational B10 and light B8 for students, MBAs and employed professionals with 5–10 years' experience. Implementation and hosted verification are in progress; this contract is not a release receipt.

## Shared candidate journey

Choose an audience and editable targets → upload and confirm source-linked career facts once → receive sourced opportunities or add a full vacancy → review evidence and unresolved conditions → inspect and approve the exact application document → download → track a dated next action → opt into bounded recurring preparation.

Audience selection changes examples and initial preferences. It never invents years of experience, professional credentials, work authorization or factual claims. Candidates may switch audience. Students use project/internship evidence, MBAs retain work and academic distinctions, and professionals control confidential disclosure and exclusions.

## External dependency failures

- Missing provider configuration: source-grounded rules mode remains available; paid AI/search readiness is false.
- Employer API failure or incomplete coverage: show source failure/coverage, retain manual vacancy intake, and never claim zero jobs in the whole market.
- Budget hold or uncertain provider timeout: retain conservative reservation; explain the hold and avoid blind paid retries.
- Missing scheduler activation: allow preparation on demand; do not advertise unattended runs as enabled.
- PDF layout/font problem: show the affected format and corrective action; preserve reviewed text and safe other formats.
- Lost authentication or stale record version: require reauthentication/review; never attach records to a caller-supplied owner.

## Data and API boundary

Existing owner-bound profile, claim, opportunity, run and packet records remain the core. Add scheduling, pilot enrollment, non-sensitive activity and support records through an additive migration. Control actions derive identity from authenticated requests; worker and scheduler dispatch have separate service authorization. Exact schemas and action types are defined in `supabase/functions/_shared/job-loop-types.ts` and the reviewed migration, rather than duplicated here.

## Exclusions

Full B8 mock interviews/case coaching, B9 coach or institution portal, applications/submissions, sent recruiter messages, connected Gmail, paid checkout, ads and outreach are excluded. No paid key activation or purchase is implied by building adapters. The owner has not authorized public claims of 98/100 readiness, 99% measured effort saving, an AI-detector percentage or employment outcomes.

Observed hosted outcomes and remaining account dependencies must be recorded separately before reporting completion.
