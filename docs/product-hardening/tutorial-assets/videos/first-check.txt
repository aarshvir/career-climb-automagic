Start with a real job you want. In Your role, enter the title from the posting. Company is optional. Select Continue to your CV. You can complete the check and download without creating an account.

Read your English CV as a text-based PDF, DOCX or TXT file, up to five megabytes, or paste the text. A scanned PDF needs readable text first. Check names, dates and headings in the extracted wording. Remove confidential information before selecting Use this reviewed CV text.

In One vacancy, paste the full job description, including responsibilities, requirements and conditions. Use the actual posting, with at least eighty characters. Select Check these inputs. The check does not open links or send an application.

Each finding shows what the vacancy asks for beside what your CV says. Related wording may still need a better example. Read the full vacancy too. Matching phrases do not verify a degree, calculate experience years or predict an interview. Select Review my CV and questions.

Read the complete CV and ask whether you could explain it in an interview. A team achievement stays a team achievement. An estimate stays an estimate. You do not need to fill every gap. Add an accurate, shareable example only if you have one, then select Recheck my changes and continue.

Read the final CV and evidence. For PDF, choose the maximum length and select Preview current PDF. If the panel is blank, open the exact preview in a new tab. Confirm accuracy and privacy, then select Approve this CV. Download PDF, DOCX or TXT. You send the document yourself.
