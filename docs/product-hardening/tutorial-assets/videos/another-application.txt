Before starting another application, download or save the approved check if you want to keep it. Under Your next application, choose which CV to reuse: Reviewed source CV or Approved final CV. The choice replaces the open check; saved applications stay unchanged.

Select Use this CV for another vacancy. Enter the new target role and optional company, review the reused CV and paste the new posting. Work through the evidence and your review again. A previous approval cannot approve this new combination of CV and vacancy.

In Job Loop, your saved career record and unchanged cleared facts are already available. Add the next real vacancy in Opportunities. Inspect available preparation on Today, then review the new document in Outputs. Every packet still has its own source checks and exact approval.

Submit on the employer site yourself. When you have actually applied, update Application status in Opportunities and confirm that you report it. Add a follow-up date and notes if helpful, then select Save tracking update. Preparing or downloading a CV does not mean an application was submitted.
