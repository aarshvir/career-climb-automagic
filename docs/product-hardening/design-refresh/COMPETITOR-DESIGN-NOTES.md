# Career-product design notes

Checked 14 October 2026. Read together with [the existing competitor benchmark](../../research/COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md). Six current official homepages were inspected through the web reader; Jobvance's `ProductPage.tsx`, `CvCheck.tsx` and header were also reviewed. This is a public content/structure review, not a signed-in usability test, pixel audit, conversion study or search-ranking comparison. Vendor outcome and popularity claims were not independently verified and are not evidence for Jobvance claims.

## What the public pages establish

| Product | Observed first-party structure | Transferable principle for Jobvance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/) | Resume creation leads the page; resume, tracker and matching views make the broader product concrete. A numbered explanation follows. | Start with a familiar task. Explain saved/reused work after showing the first useful output. |
| [Huntr](https://huntr.co/) | A compact introductory promise, a free-start action and introductory video precede distinct tracker, builder and tailoring sections with product images. | Give one immediate action and a secondary explanation. Use real product examples to carry detail. |
| [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/) | Its first action is a resume scan. The page presents a match-report image and separate examples of keyword and resume feedback. | Show the report a visitor will receive. Jobvance should explain passages and questions rather than imply that a score verifies qualifications. |
| [Rezi](https://www.rezi.ai/) | A resume upload/drop prompt appears near the hero. The next explanation distinguishes building, scoring and targeting, with editor and document images. | Put the user's material close to the starting action. Keep the destination visible and the number of decisions small. |
| [Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/) | Resume building and checking are distinct actions. Template choices and resume customization appear early, followed by specific editing features. | Make document quality visible. A CV preview is more relevant than abstract artwork; advanced controls can appear later. |
| [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com/en/) | A short resume-focused headline and free-start action lead into builder, checking and tailoring sections. Template previews have explicit preview/use actions. | Use short task language and show a recognizable document. Each preview should have an obvious next action. |

These observations support product-demonstration and task-clarity ideas, not copying any company's colors, wording, artwork, layout or logo. No result establishes a superior product, market leader, conversion lift or SEO advantage.

## Recommended direction

1. **Give the hero one job.** A direct heading such as “A CV prepared for your next role.”, one sentence explaining CV plus vacancy, and one primary “Check my CV” action. Keep “See an example” as the quiet secondary link. The repeated existing phrases “clearer”, “next chapter” and “room to prepare” create mood without explaining the work.
2. **Show a believable output.** Present one legible document excerpt beside one source requirement, with a clear original/reviewed relationship. Mark invented example material “Fictional example” at the point of viewing. Do not add invented ratings, clients, testimonials, success percentages or polished fake product controls.
3. **Explain three phases while respecting the six actual screens.** “Add your details → Review the CV → Download.” Under “Add”, say that users supply a role, CV and vacancy. The current workflow has six screens; do not advertise three clicks or a completion time that was not measured.
4. **Use personas to reduce explanation, not add setup.** Students see projects/internships; MBA candidates see prior work and their own project contribution; professionals see existing work and transferable examples. Choosing a persona should stay optional and editable. Never infer employment, ownership or outcomes from that choice.
5. **Keep the first form economical.** Ask for the CV and role/vacancy material needed for the check. Company stays optional. Offer upload and paste at the same level. Put “No finished CV? Start with your facts” beside that input. Defer account creation, saving, recurring preparation, inbox access and billing until the relevant action. Preserve explicit review and permission controls where they affect saved data or final claims.
6. **Make details available at the decision.** Near input: short local-check explanation. Near saving: what is saved and where. Near approval: accuracy and sensitive-information choices. Use expandable detail for full explanations instead of repeating long paragraphs across the hero and every stage. Never generalize the local guest check into a local-only claim for the cloud workspace.

## Original visual proposal

This is an original design recommendation, not a claim about competitor palettes. Use warm paper `#F7F4EE`, deep aubergine/ink `#2C2033`, muted plum `#735C78`, and restrained burnt coral `#B94932`. Coral is an action accent; body copy should remain dark. Verify contrast in the finished page.

Use a decisive typographic hierarchy, generous breathing room, thin dividers, and a document-shaped preview. Reserve an editorial serif for a small number of large headings; keep UI labels and form controls in a readable sans. Favor a few intentional section compositions over many repeated rounded cards. Avoid gradients, orbit shapes, glowing accents, scattered badges and decorative mini-dashboards. A compact original folded-page/J symbol could connect the brand to the CV task more specifically than the current rising-arrow mark; check small-size legibility and do not imply trademark clearance.

## Plain-language replacements

| Existing direction | More concrete copy |
| --- | --- |
| “Your CV. One vacancy. A clearer application.” | “Prepare your CV for the role you want.” |
| “Your next chapter” | “Your first role”, “Your post-MBA move”, or “A move alongside work”, where relevant. |
| “Follow the words back to their source.” | “Compare the job requirements with your CV.” |
| “Your final call. Your document to send.” | “Review your CV, then approve the download.” |
| “Your working draft. Your choice to save.” | “Download now. Save to your account if you want.” |

These are editorial proposals. None implies AI-detector evasion, verified qualifications, guaranteed interviews, employment outcomes or measured time savings.

## Refresh acceptance checks

- A new visitor can identify what to bring, what they receive and where to start without reading the full page.
- At 390 px, the headline, explanatory sentence and primary action remain readable; product-preview text is legible rather than ornamental.
- Every visible button works, each form has a clear next action, and returning to a previous stage keeps the entered material within the documented draft behavior.
- Personas affect guidance and examples without adding mandatory questions.
- Fictional examples are labelled; verified product behavior supplies the proof.
- Screen-reader labels, visible keyboard focus, contrast and reduced-motion preferences remain intact.
