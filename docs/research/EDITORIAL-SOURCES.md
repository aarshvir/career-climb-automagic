# Jobvance guide library: source and editorial record

Prepared 13 October 2026. Publisher: **Jobvance Editorial**. The guides were prepared with AI assistance and source checks by the implementation agent. No named human adviser, legal reviewer, customer interview, customer outcome or expert credential is claimed. The public disclosure describes that process without inventing a human byline.

The library contains 20 original task-focused guides for students, MBA candidates and busy professionals, primarily in a UAE application-preparation context. Each has a distinct intent, five substantive sections, a clearly labelled fictional example, practical next actions and relevant links. The body of each guide exceeds 500 words; this is an internal completeness check, not a claim that Google requires a particular word count. Most text is original workflow analysis and editorial suggestions. Short source-backed statements do not imply that the linked institution endorses Jobvance.

## Search guidance used for editorial review

[Google Search Central's generative-AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) was opened directly; its displayed update date was 1 October 2026. It calls for accuracy, quality, relevance, review of generated material and useful creation context. [Google's helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) was also opened. The library follows its reader-focused questions by adding original decision processes rather than rewriting source articles. These documents do not establish ranking, indexing, traffic or AI-answer inclusion for Jobvance.

## Checks and remaining limits

Content integrity tests verify 20 unique slugs/titles, at least 500 body words per article, serializability, unique paragraphs, fictional-example labels, valid related-guide references, HTTPS source URLs, valid section citation IDs, accurate reading-time calculation and honest publisher disclosure. UI tests verify labelled search/filters, keyboard navigation, useful empty-state recovery, article section targets, citations, visible full content, free-check CTA and no-index recovery for unknown guides.

The preparation process does not substitute for a professional career review or individual legal advice. No real users were interviewed and no application outcomes were measured. Source checks are bounded to the pages above, and some UAE pages were available only through official search extracts. All legal/visa/certificate claims are narrow, cited and direct the reader to current official requirements. The root implementation separately verifies route metadata, initial crawlable HTML, sitemap, navigation and production build. Those checks demonstrate implementation readiness, not search performance.
