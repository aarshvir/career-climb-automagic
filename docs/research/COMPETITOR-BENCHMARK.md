# Six-platform public competitor benchmark

Checked 13 October 2026, using current primary product, pricing, help and privacy pages. This is a public-journey comparison, not an authenticated product test. No sign-up, account assumption, upload, purchase or application was made. Advertised features are reported as vendor claims. Prices are displayed USD prices; checkout, taxes, country pricing and account-specific offers were not verified.

There is no traffic dataset, Search Console data, independent usage measurement or comparable search-ranking study here. Therefore this document does not identify the most-used platform or the strongest SEO performer. Crawlable text and resource links are observable; traffic, index coverage and outcomes are unknown.

Jobvance's local guest check and separate save choices can be a clear point of differentiation **if behavior remains consistent with the published explanation**. Keep the short near-input statement specific to the check. The signed-in cloud workflow needs its own plain description of what is saved, sent to a selected provider, connected, deleted or left in separately saved records. Do not generalize local-only handling to the whole product.

For Jobvance, finish the current public routes with unique task descriptions, honest examples, internal next-action links and real recovery answers. Verify served public HTML, canonical URLs, sitemap and private-route indexing boundaries. Measure Search Console coverage later. Creating hundreds of generic pages is not justified by this review.

## Product decisions from the comparison

1. Make a prepared application the first outcome, with a concise source comparison rather than an unexplained score. The vendors' broad feature catalogs do not require Jobvance to add every feature.
2. Make saved career facts and the second vacancy concrete. Teal and Huntr publicly explain reusable resume/work-history workflows; Jobvance should explain and verify its equivalent reuse path.
3. Provide a starter outline for students and clear distinctions for MBA/professional evidence. A persona selector should adjust examples, not invent qualifications or force lengthy setup.
4. Add task-specific help and a usable contact path. A short screen-recorded walkthrough can support these answers, but the text path must be complete.
5. Preserve accurate pricing and capability boundaries: Jobvance's proposed paid prices are not current purchase offers; free pilot access and configuration-dependent services should be labelled at the action point.

All five are product inferences to test, not observed conversion lifts. A fresh timed pilot and independent reviewer should decide whether the built experience delivers them.
