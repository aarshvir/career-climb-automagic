# Jobvance: product manager priorities

Reviewed 13 October 2026. Baseline source: commit `f225451`. This review deliberately did not read previous audit grades or use a target grade. The live homepage and guest check were inspected independently with fictional input. No account, payment, outreach, application or third-party avatar generation was performed.

## Decision

Make one common job-to-be-done excellent: **use experience I can explain to prepare one application, then reuse that experience for the next vacancy without rebuilding my record**. Start with a CV or pasted career text, one vacancy, visible evidence, an editable review and a downloadable document. Saving and an ongoing Job Loop are the next value after the first useful result.

Students, MBA candidates and professionals with 5–10 years of experience need different examples and guidance, not three separate systems. A student's coursework must stay coursework. An MBA team project must stay distinct from previous employment. A professional's supporting contribution must not become leadership, and confidential employer/client details need a clear way to stay out of the record.

This scope is a hypothesis about the shared need, based on the requested audiences and the existing product. It is not a finding from customer interviews. Competitor repetition is evidence of a market pattern, not proof that Jobvance users want every competitor feature. See [the competitor benchmark](COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md).

## Ranked work and practical acceptance checks

Estimates below assume an engineer familiar with this source and include focused implementation plus verification. They are planning ranges, not committed schedules. Reach is expressed as an affected task, because measured audience volumes and funnel analytics were not supplied. Doubling the effort estimates still leaves the first three items ahead of new integrations in this scope.

| Rank | Fix and affected task | Reason for priority | Estimated effort / confidence | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One first route with a clear result and visible starting input | All new visitors need to understand what they bring, what they receive and whether an account is needed. Existing guest entry is already implemented. | 0.5–1.5 days; high confidence in inconsistency, medium in conversion effect | Home/header/audience CTAs consistently offer the first application check. In a 20-second comprehension test, a new participant can explain the inputs, reviewed output, price and account requirement. For a student who has no CV, start with a compact factual outline: education, project, own contribution, internship/volunteering and any accurate result. A one-click fictional example can explain the structure, but must not silently insert fictional achievements into a real record. An elaborate template library is not required to validate this need.

## First-value target and pilot

**Five minutes is the requested design target, not a proven result or a public promise.** With a readable CV/career outline and a copied vacancy ready, the target is to reach a source-linked review, understand one supported example and one unresolved point, then obtain an approved usable download within five minutes. Measure understanding and export success as well as speed; clicking through without reading does not satisfy the target.

Use a small formative pilot, for example nine consenting adults: three students, three MBA candidates and three professionals with 5–10 years of experience. No recruitment or outreach was performed. Give each person two realistic vacancies and redacted or fictional source material. Observe an unassisted first application, a second application with reused facts, and one recovery task. Use counterbalanced vacancies so the second attempt is not always easier.

Record homepage-to-first-review time, first-review-to-download time, errors/assistance, source comprehension, failed export, and second-application effort. Proposed learning gates: at least 7/9 complete the first task unaided; median prepared-input completion at or below five minutes; no participant submits an invented achievement; at least 7/9 find reuse without prompting; second-application median at least 30% faster than the first. These are provisional acceptance targets for learning, not statistical population estimates. Report each audience separately alongside raw counts and failures. Do not infer increased interviews from this pilot.

The observer should ask participants to explain what is saved locally, what is saved to the account, and whether anything was sent. Collect task events without career text, email addresses or employer-sensitive content. Permission for any real documents, recording or analytics must be handled separately. A seven-day voluntary return can test whether the reused source remains useful; it does not need an automatic email campaign to start.

## Defer until the common workflow is validated

Defer broader job-board coverage, autonomous application submission, extensive LinkedIn integrations, a large resume-template catalog, interview coaching, salary negotiation, a native mobile app and a broad SEO content factory. Existing capabilities can remain available, but they should not become prerequisites for first value. Any later feature should show a measurable bottleneck in the first or second application task.

The next independent reviewer should receive the built public URL and neutral task instructions, not these proposed solutions or prior grades. Compare their observations with actual acceptance evidence after their review, and record unresolved issues honestly.
