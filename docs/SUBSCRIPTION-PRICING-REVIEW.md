# Subscription pricing review

Reviewed 11 October 2026, Asia/Dubai. These are proposed commercial tiers. The current beta remains free, checkout is disabled and a pilot does not convert automatically to a paid subscription.

## Primary-source benchmarks

- [Teal pricing](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing): unlimited free résumé creation/tracking; Teal+ advertised at US$29 per 30 days, US$13 per seven days and US$79 per 90 days.
- [Huntr pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing): free base résumés/PDF export, limited tailored packets and a tracker; Pro advertised at US$40 monthly, US$90 quarterly and US$160 per six months.

These advertised offers establish competitive context. They do not prove Jobvance's conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, retention, willingness to pay or margin. Provider claims about interview outcomes were not adopted.

## Recommended proposed tiers

| Tier | Proposed monthly price | Intended use | Proposed allowance |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free Starter | US$0 | Check an existing CV against a vacancy and understand the next action. | Guest/manual entry tools; no promise of free unlimited paid AI or broad discovery. |
| Focus | US$15 | Students, MBAs and focused applicants bringing selected vacancies. | 20 prepared application packs; manual preparation and tracking. |
| Job Loop | US$29 | Busy employed professionals and active MBA searches needing recurring preparation. | Up to 30 scheduled runs and 60 prepared packs; maximum two new packs in one run. |

Audience does not determine an assumed ability to pay or require unimplemented student verification. Applicants choose a plan based on their workflow. All ten enrolled beta users receive the available pilot loop at US$0, subject to explicit bounded use and the shared budget.

Do not sell an annual contract or an unlimited-AI promise before measuring repeated application use and useful-pack operating cost. Search is episodic: cancellation/pause and eventually a clearly priced short search period should be tested rather than assuming year-long retention.

## Cost boundary

The pilot's hard ceiling is US$50 in Jobvance-accounted AI/search usage per UTC month, with at most ten active enrolled users. Rules-based fact selection and employer public API reads do not consume paid-model/search credits. Existing free hosting remains best effort; this ceiling is not an enforceable cap on unrelated accounts, a hosting subscription, domain renewal, taxes or a provider's changed price.

Before enabling a paid model, configure dedicated keys/credits and verified model prices. Reservations must include maximum input/output, failed or uncertain calls and retries. Show held/exhausted states. Quotas limit work; they are not evidence of cost until real invoices and useful approved packs are reconciled.

## Commercial validation still needed

Observe consenting users completing first and second applications. Separate setup/review time from idle runtime, record assistance and failures, and measure repeat useful packs. Test price choices without charging or contacting anyone under this build request. Billing activation later requires an eligible merchant setup and independently tested checkout, entitlements, cancellation/refunds and reconciliation.
