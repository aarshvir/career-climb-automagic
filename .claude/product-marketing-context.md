# Jobvance marketing context

Owner-provided target: university students/new graduates, MBA students/pivots, and employed professionals with5–10years of experience who have limited time. Primarily UAE context in current research; target locations remain editable. A shared workflow matters more than separate products for every persona.

Product: evidence-based application preparation. Start with an actual CV and one vacancy, compare requirements with original source passages, review/reorder the wording, approve an exact PDF/DOCX/TXT export, optionally save a reusable private career record and track user-reported progress. Job Loop adds available employer-source discovery, confirmed-fact reuse, separate packets, optional source-based recurring preparation and dated actions.

Primary first value: one relevant, reviewable application document without creating an account. Signup is for private saving/reuse/cloud operation. Desired activation and time reduction are hypotheses to measure with people, not claims already proved by tests.

Positioning: prepare your next application without rebuilding your CV or losing the evidence. Avoid a generic “AI gets you hired” promise. Explain the next click, what is automatic, what needs review and what the user receives.

Constraints: free ten-person pilot; no card or paid checkout. Future proposed Focus$15/month and Job Loop$29/month are not current purchases/entitlements. Shared AI/search cap$50 per UTCmonth. External models use explicit per-run consent; raw source and confidential information require care. Existing schedules stay rules-based. Free employer feeds are bounded sources, not the entire market. Paid broad search and mailbox integration are not currently connected. The user approves documents and sends/applies on employer sites. No automatic application/send promise, hiring guarantee, ATS-pass guarantee, fake success metrics or guaranteed search ranking.

Brand voice: clear, practical, calm, human and precise; useful source examples labelled fictional. Show an actual path and fewer forms. Do not invent human reviewers, customer quotes, expert credentials, human avatars or completed videos. AI-assisted editorial material discloses its process.

SEO: useful original guides organized by customer task, accurate page titles/descriptions and canonical routes, text present in initial HTML, internal links, clear authorship, primary sources, sitemap, mobile accessibility and private-page no-index. Readiness does not prove ranking, traffic or inclusion in Google/Gemini/ChatGPT/Claude answers.

Competitive comparison underway: Teal, Huntr, Jobscan, Rezi, Resume Worded and Careerflow. Use current official evidence; no invented competitors' traffic, outcomes or unseen authenticated behavior. Draft context grounded in the user's instructions and current code; no customer interviews have been claimed.
